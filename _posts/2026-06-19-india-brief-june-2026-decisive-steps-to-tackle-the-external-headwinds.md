# India Brief – June 2026

We are thrilled to launch the June edition of our video series **'India Brief'** – house view on equity and fixed income markets.

## Key Takeaways

- Elevated oil prices and the potential impact from El Nino continue to be headwinds for India, leading us to downgrade FY27 GDP growth forecast to 6.0%.
- We retain our tactical mild underweight stance on Indian equities.
- RBI policy actions might help boost the sentiment in near term.
