## The Great Cyber-Threat Landscape

In 2023, India recorded over 79 million cyberattacks, becoming the third-most targeted country for phishing globally—following the US and the UK. This marked a 15% increase from the previous year.

The surge continued into 2024. In the first quarter alone, over 500 million cyberattacks were intercepted, and Indians faced financial losses exceeding INR 1,750 crore.

This alarming rise in cybercrimes has made cybersecurity a non-negotiable aspect for not just institutions but also individuals.

It is no longer about "if" you'll be targeted. It is a matter of "how" you should safeguard yourself.

While cybersecurity itself is a very vast universe, today we'll focus on the key areas that matter most for protecting your wealth and identity.

## 1. Cyber-Security Threats You Should Know

Cybercriminals use a variety of tactics to target individuals. Some of the most common threats include:

- **Phishing:** Fraudulent messages or emails that appear legitimate, tricking you into sharing personal information or clicking malicious links.
- **Vishing and Smishing:** Voice calls and SMS-based scams that create a sense of urgency to manipulate you into revealing sensitive data.
- **Identity Theft:** Stealing your personal information to access your accounts or commit fraud in your name.
- **Ransomware:** Malware that locks your files or system until you pay a ransom.

## 2. How to Secure Your Personal Data and Digital Identity

Protecting your digital identity is the first line of defense. Follow these best practices:

- Use strong, unique passwords for every account, and consider a password manager.
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
- Regularly review your privacy settings on social media and banking apps.
- Avoid sharing personal details (like your date of birth or address) publicly online.
- Be cautious of unsolicited messages asking for OTPs, PINs, or passwords.

## 3. How to Secure Your Banking and Financial Transactions

Your bank accounts are a prime target. Here's how to keep them safe:

- Always verify the authenticity of any payment link or request, even if it appears to come from a known contact.
- Use only official banking apps and websites, and avoid transacting on public Wi-Fi.
- Set up transaction alerts and regularly monitor your bank statements.
- Never share card details, CVV, or OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank.

## 4. How to Secure Your Investments

Investment accounts hold significant value, making them attractive to cybercriminals. To safeguard them:

- Use strong, separate passwords for your investment and demat accounts.
- Opt for biometric or hardware-based authentication if available.
- Keep your brokerage contact details updated so you receive alerts about any account activity.
- Regularly check your portfolio for unauthorized changes.

## 5. Cyber Insurance to Protect Your Wealth

Cyber insurance can offer financial protection against losses from cybercrimes, including fraud and identity theft. Consider the following:

- Review your existing insurance policies to see if they cover cyber-related losses.
- Compare cyber insurance plans and understand what they do and don't cover.
- Look for policies that include identity restoration services and legal assistance.

## Conclusion

In today's hyper-digital world, cybersecurity is not optional—it's essential. By understanding the risks and implementing protective measures, you can significantly reduce your vulnerability. Stay vigilant, verify before you act, and make cybersecurity a habit, not an afterthought.
