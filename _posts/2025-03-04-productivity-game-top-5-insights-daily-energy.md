**Favorite Insight:**

In the book *Breath*, James Nestor reviews centuries of breathing practices and extensive scientific research on breathing. He concludes that a **5.5-second inhale followed by an immediate 5.5-second exhale** (repeated for several minutes) is the ideal rhythm for maximizing cellular energy.

You can optimize your 5.5-second breaths by breathing in through your nose. Nasal breathing purifies, heats, moistens, and pressurizes the air, increasing oxygen absorption by **10 to 15%**. Nasal breathing also boosts nitric oxide production by **six times**, which improves blood circulation and further increases your energy.
