Six years ago, I became a first-time founder. This year, I've become a first-time author.

Around three years ago, I started thinking deeply about what it really takes to become a millionaire. It's not just about money—it's about mindset, discipline, and a long-term vision. I've learned that the path is rarely linear, and the most important investments are often in yourself.

In my experience, the real turning point came when I stopped focusing on shortcuts and started embracing the slow, steady work of building something meaningful. Whether you're starting a company, writing a book, or simply changing your spending habits, the principles remain the same.

This newsletter is my attempt to share those principles with you—the frameworks and lessons that have guided me through the ups and downs of entrepreneurship and personal finance. I hope they help you on your own journey, whatever that may look like.
