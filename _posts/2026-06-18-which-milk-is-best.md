You might have switched to oat milk at some point, Amol. Maybe for the environment, maybe because it genuinely tastes better in a flat white. Both are great reasons. But here is something worth knowing about what it does to your glucose.

**The oat milk problem**

When oat milk is produced, manufacturers add enzymes to break down the oat starch into smaller sugars (like maltose). It's an industrial process called “enzyming”, and it's why many store-bought oat milks taste naturally sweeter than homemade oat milk: the starch has basically been broken down into sugar. [(see an example process)](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KVDGBC1W0F9ARDAMZHMZ07XY_1)

And because the carbohydrate is already "pre-digested" into smaller sugars, it hits your bloodstream faster, especially on an empty stomach.
