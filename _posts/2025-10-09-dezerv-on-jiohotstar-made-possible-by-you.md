We have some news to share—and it's only possible because of you.

Dezerv's journey has been featured in Season 2 of Bharat Fintech Story, streaming on JioHotstar.

Being part of this series—alongside some of India's most respected financial institutions—is an honor.

But what makes this truly special is that the episode features our clients' voices, experiences, and trust in what we're building together.

When Sahil, Vaibhav, and I started this in 2021, we had one clear mission: to fix what's broken in wealth management. To build complete alignment with our clients. To deliver superior client-experience through technology. To earn trust through transparency.

**To all our clients who have entrusted us to manage their wealth—over INR 14,000 crore of it:** This milestone belongs to you. Your trust drives everything we do.

**To users who track their wealth on the Dezerv App:** Thank you for your continuous feedback. It has made the Dezerv App a favorite amongst wealth creators to track and understand their wealth better.

The work continues: helping you manage and grow your wealth with confidence, clarity, and complete peace of mind.
