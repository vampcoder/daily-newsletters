It's 2am here, and the world seems *so quiet*. I’m feeding this tiny person who clearly hasn't understood yet that nights are for sleeping...

Postpartum keeps reminding me to **be kind to myself**. To accept that things will be different for a little while. And I know my body is still recovering.

Because one day I’m starving and reaching for every food in my pantry. The next day I forget to eat lunch. Also I’m constantly thirsty and walking around everywhere with my giant waterbottle. And sometimes I’m hyperfocused and can get so much done, and other times I don’t even remember why I opened a new tab on my browser.

This pretty much sums up parenthood for me right now 🤪 coffee coffee coffee

Research shows that after pregnancy, the **structure of a woman’s brain** can take up to 2 years to return back to normal. *2 YEARS!*

And many other body systems also need time to fully recover: hormones, metabolism, muscles, the pelvic floor all take months (sometimes years) to settle back after pregnancy, which is why **the real postpartum period is much longer than people think**.

It's great to have this info, and reassuring in a way to know that I’m not alone in this. BUT then I ask myself: ***“ok, so what do I do with this now?”***
