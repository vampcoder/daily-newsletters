### DeepMind Fired Its Nobel Team

Less than a year after AlphaFold won Google DeepMind a Nobel Prize, the company has reportedly let go of the team responsible for the breakthrough. The move points to a strategic pivot away from long-horizon fundamental research and toward shipping commercial products. For those watching the industry, it's a stark signal about where Alphabet wants DeepMind's talent—and money—to go next.

### Disney's Copilot Era Ends

The much-hyped experiment with a Disney-branded AI copilot is over. The entertainment giant has quietly sunset the initiative, joining a growing list of companies pulling back from standalone assistants that promised to do everything and delivered little. The lesson appears to be that AI works best inside a clear workflow, not as a lifestyle companion.

### Claude Has No Bottom Line

Anthropic's Claude has been lauded for its nuanced handling of sensitive topics, but the company recently admitted that the model's behavior has no fixed, inviolable ethical floor. Instead, Claude's principles are dynamically tuned to the context—a pragmatic choice, but one that raises uncomfortable questions about where the line actually is, and who gets to move it.
