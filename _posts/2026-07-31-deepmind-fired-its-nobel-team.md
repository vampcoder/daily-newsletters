## DeepMind Fired Its Nobel Team

**👀 What's happening:** Less than a year after [AlphaFold won Google DeepMind a Nobel Prize](https://aisecret.us/r/9481460c?m=0e4f1869-00e1-41fa-a8af-6), the company has disbanded the team responsible for the breakthrough.
