## Nifty Next 50: Should It Be In Your Portfolio?

When we talk about investing in indices, Nifty 50 and Sensex usually take the spotlight. But there is another index that often goes unnoticed – the **Nifty Next 50**. This index tracks the 50 companies that are ranked just after the Nifty 50 in terms of market capitalisation. These are not small companies; they are the next big names that have the potential to grow into tomorrow's large caps.

Including Nifty Next 50 in your portfolio can offer a good balance between growth and safety. Since these companies are already established but not yet part of the top 50, they often have higher growth potential compared to the large caps. At the same time, they are more stable than small caps, making them an attractive middle ground for investors who want to diversify without taking on too much risk.

If you are building a long-term portfolio, it is worth considering how the Nifty Next 50 fits into your asset allocation strategy. The key is to look at your investment horizon, risk appetite, and how this index complements your existing holdings.

## ITR Filing Checklist

With the income tax return (ITR) filing season underway, it can be easy to miss a step. Here is a quick checklist to make the process smooth and error-free:

- **Keep all necessary documents ready** – Form 16 (if you are a salaried employee), Form 26AS, bank statements, and investment proofs.
- **Verify your Aadhaar and PAN** – ensure they are linked and updated.
- **Choose the correct ITR form** – based on your income sources and type.
- **Report all income sources** – salary, capital gains, interest, rental income, and any other earnings.
- **Claim all eligible deductions** – like those under Section 80C, 80D, and home loan interest.
- **Reconcile with Form 26AS** – check for any mismatches between your TDS and income.
- **E-file and verify** – once you submit, verify your return using Aadhaar OTP, net banking, or other options.

Going through this checklist can help you file your return correctly and on time, avoiding unnecessary notices from the tax department.

## Inside the Latest Mutual Fund Portfolios

The latest mutual fund portfolios tell different stories. While **Parag Parikh Flexi Cap** added more **Infosys**, **Nippon India Small Cap** exited it. Infosys wasn't the only stock they disagreed on. Such differences highlight how fund managers interpret market conditions and company prospects.

For investors, this is a reminder that there is no one-size-fits-all approach. Different funds have different strategies, and what works for one manager may not work for another. It is important to look at a fund's portfolio to understand its style and whether it aligns with your own investment philosophy.
