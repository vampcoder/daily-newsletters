Let’s do a glucose-steady advent calendar!

I know end-of-the-year can be the most difficult time to do the hacks and keep our glucose steady. All the dinner parties, the family travel, the reunions with friends, and the freezing cold weather. (okay maybe not in Australia but still...)

So to help you navigate this chocolate-filled period, I’m doing an advent calendar of FREE recipes to keep you glucose steady!

I’m sharing this recipe first because it’s a total game-changer: it’s like dessert for breakfast, but it’ll keep you steady for the whole day.

This is because it contains **20g of protein** (*from the yogurt, almonds, and sunflower seeds*) and nothing sweet except whole fruit. Plus, I usually add another **20g of unsweetened protein powder** *(I use unflavored whey isolate*) to the crumble, to hit my daily protein goal.
