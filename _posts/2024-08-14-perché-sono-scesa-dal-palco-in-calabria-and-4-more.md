# Why I Stepped Off Stage in Calabria

*By Selvaggia Lucarelli*

I want to explain what happened that evening in Calabria, because too much has been said already, and most of it isn't true.

I was invited to speak at an event. I had prepared my notes, I was ready to talk about the topics I usually discuss. But when I arrived, I realized something was off. The atmosphere was different from what I had been promised. There were people in the room who had been publicly hostile to me and to the values I stand for. I made a quick decision: I would not go on stage.

I stepped down. Some people saw it as an act of courage, others as an insult to the organizers and the audience. I understand both readings, but I need to clarify why I did it.

It wasn't about disrespecting the people who had come to listen. It was about sending a message: I won't share a stage with people who, in my view, have crossed a line. I have always believed that words matter, and that we should be responsible for the company we keep. That night, I couldn't reconcile my conscience with the situation.

I know that some will say I overreacted, that I should have stayed and voiced my disagreement. But I've learned that sometimes the most honest answer is to walk away. I don't expect everyone to agree. I just ask that you hear me out.
