The latest episode of *Solved* just dropped: ***Love on Trial***.

We're told love is the answer—to loneliness, to purpose, to happiness. But in today's dating world, love can feel more like a minefield. Between the dopamine highs, guessing games, and swipe fatigue, it's easy to wonder: is love still worth it?

In this episode, Drew and I take love to court. We unpack the messy psychology behind modern romance. From chemistry that hijacks your brain to the attachment patterns that quietly run the show, we challenge the myths, look at the science, and get honest about what healthy love actually requires.

You'll walk away with a sharper lens for viewing love and attraction, and how to weigh its costs and benefits in your life. We all value love. The question is how much of your time and attention does it really deserve?
