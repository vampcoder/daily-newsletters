Amol, am I the only who just wants to stuff my face with 20 cookies right now?

**It is SO cold and grey already where I am.** I walked out without a coat yesterday (*big mistake*) and my body was *instantly* craving cookies. (okay it doesn’t help that I have this amazing specialty cookie shop right next to my appartement…)

So I started wondering… **what is actually happening in my body and brain right now?** And it turns out the science is so interesting! It’s not a weakness for carbs or a lack of willpower. **It’s a natural biological response.**

Here's the science: as the days get shorter and light levels drop, our brain produces **less serotonin**, the “feel-good” neurotransmitter. To boost it back up, our body starts craving quick sources of energy (*hello, cookies!*) because **eating carbs helps increase serotonin production**. ([ready study](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01K83R23P1TVFR63E3NAR899PC_1))

At the same time, colder temperatures signal your metabolism to **conserve heat and energy**, nudging you to reach for comforting, calorie-dense foods.

In other words, my cookie craving is my biology trying to keep me *happy & warm.*

BUT... as you know, carb-heavy comfort food will only make us feel better *temporarily*: it’s a very **short-term solution.** After that quick serotonin hit, your **glucose crashes and you feel even more sluggishness.** Ugh!

So instead of fighting the cravings (*or giving in and feeling terrible afterwards*), my goal is to focus on **easy hacks to keep my energy and mood stable**
