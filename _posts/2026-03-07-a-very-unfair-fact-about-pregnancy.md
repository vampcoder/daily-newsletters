Here’s one of the most unfair facts about pregnancy: **The exact same food creates a much bigger glucose spike when you’re pregnant than when you’re not.**

As pregnancy progresses, your body becomes naturally more insulin-resistant, meaning glucose stays higher for longer. What makes this even trickier is that pregnancy doesn’t just change how your body handles sugar, it also **changes how your brain responds to it**.

Many women notice that sugar tastes better during pregnancy, and cravings feel stronger. Scientists have been puzzled by this for years, but animal studies offer a clue: in pregnant rats, the same amount of sugar triggers a **stronger reward and pleasure response in the brain**.

And that actually made sense thousands of years ago: very little sugar around, only some seasonal fruit, so eating any sugar we could find meant a survival advantage for our baby. But today, when 15 donuts can arrive at your door in under 5 minutes, this ancient wiring may actually be working against us.

And here’s the part that surprises almost everyone I speak to: In the third trimester, your baby only needs **about 35 grams of glucose per day**.
