Whenever I talk about building better habits, people tell me the same thing: *"I start strong… and then life gets in the way."*

It’s not usually motivation that’s missing. It’s visibility. Without a simple way to see your progress, it’s too easy to forget a habit for a few days, then a week, and before you know it, you’re starting from scratch.

That’s why I created the **Habit Tracker**—a simple Google Sheet that makes it ridiculously easy to track the habits you want to build and see your progress at a glance. Each day, you just tick off whether you completed the habit. Over time, those ticks become a chain you don’t want to break.

Inside the Habit Tracker, you’ll be able to:

* **List your key habits** so you know exactly what you’re working on.
* **Track daily completions** with a quick check.
* **Spot patterns** that show when and why you miss a habit, so you can fix the root cause.
* **Build streaks** that turn “I should do this” into “I don’t miss this.”

It’s simple, but that’s the point. Tools don’t have to be complicated to work. They just need to make the right action the easy action. I use a version of this myself, and it’s amazing how a single visual record can keep you accountable long after the initial burst of motivation fades.

You can grab the Habit Tracker and start building your streak today.
