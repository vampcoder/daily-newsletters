The latest episode of *Solved* just dropped, and it’s a little different than what we’ve been doing. Instead of focusing on a topic, I answered questions from inside *The Solved Community.*

What kind of questions? Ones like:

*“How do I reduce the spiraling feelings of existential dread when I have to spend the majority of my time on activities that are NOT aligned with my purpose?”*

And: *“How do I know what my REAL purpose is?”*

To hear the answers to these questions and more, listen to the latest episode. Even if you don’t have the exact same questions, the answers may help you get yourself out of any seasonal existential dread you’re personally experiencing.
