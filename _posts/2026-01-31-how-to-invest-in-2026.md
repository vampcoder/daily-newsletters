Earlier this month, we hosted Decipher with Dezerv in Hyderabad — our flagship investor conference, now in its newest city. The format is simple: bring together some of India's sharpest investment minds and ask them one question: how should you invest from here?

Four experts, with a combined ₹1 lakh crore in assets under management, shared their perspectives on the year ahead. Here are the key themes that emerged.

### 1. The Case for Active Management

One panelist argued that in a market with high dispersion, active managers have a distinct edge. Rather than relying on index funds, they emphasized the importance of stock picking to uncover opportunities that aren't yet reflected in the broader market.

### 2. Asset Allocation Matters More Than Ever

Another expert stressed that asset allocation is the primary driver of long-term returns. They recommended a balanced portfolio across equities, debt, and gold, and warned against trying to time the market. Instead, they suggested periodic rebalancing to stay aligned with one's risk profile.

### 3. Quality Over Valuation

A third panelist focused on the quality of businesses — strong management, clean balance sheets, and sustainable cash flows. They advised investors to be willing to pay a premium for quality, even if it means holding through short-term volatility.

### 4. The India Growth Story Remains Intact

Finally, the fourth expert highlighted India's structural growth drivers: demographic dividends, digital adoption, and infrastructure spending. They recommended that investors focus on sectors that are direct beneficiaries of these long-term trends, such as financial services, manufacturing, and technology.

In the Q&A session that followed, the panel addressed common investor pitfalls, such as overconcentration in a single stock or sector, and offered practical advice on how to build a resilient portfolio for the coming years.

As we look toward 2026, the consensus is clear: stay disciplined, stay diversified, and focus on the long term.
