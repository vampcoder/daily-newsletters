**"Success is the lifelong experiment of discovering what makes you feel most alive."** – Anne-Laure Le Cunff

In this book summary, you'll learn how **Tiny Experiments**—small, time-bound, low-risk projects—can help you uncover a more fulfilling career path without upending your life.

**Favorite Takeaway:**

Each Tiny Experiment is:

- **Time-bound** (15–100 days)
- **Output-focused** (create something daily or weekly with zero reward expectations—no chasing views or likes)
- **Iterative** (start simple, improve slightly each time)

The key is to select a tiny experiment based on something you want to learn and explore, and then commit to a short time period (even 100 days is a blip in the span of your working life) where you're bound to learn something new about yourself. The downside is minimal, but the upside could change your life. You might discover something that looks like work to others but feels like play to you. You may also find your tribe—people who want to hear what you say.
