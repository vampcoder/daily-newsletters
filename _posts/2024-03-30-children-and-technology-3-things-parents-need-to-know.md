Social Psychologist Jonathan Haidt’s latest book, *The Anxious Generation*, is about how technology and changing parenting styles relate to an alarming rise in mental illness among teens. Is social media really *causing* the teen mental health crisis? While our viewpoints may differ, we are very aligned on the conclusions for what’s to be done to help our kids.

Here we offer three commonsense, research-supported approaches for helping kids learn to control their use of technology instead of letting it control them.
