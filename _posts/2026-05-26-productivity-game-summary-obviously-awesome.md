**“Positioning is the act of deliberately defining how you are the best at something that a defined market cares a lot about.” – April Dunford**

In this summary, you'll learn how to position anything you sell so **more people notice, care, and buy.**

**Favorite Takeaway:**

Choose a positioning strategy that will help you stand out:

1. Be superior in ONE way customers really care about.
2. Be the big fish in a small pond (own a subsegment, like "Podcasts for 4-Hour Side Hustles").
3. Create an entirely new category (like 5-Hour Energy).
