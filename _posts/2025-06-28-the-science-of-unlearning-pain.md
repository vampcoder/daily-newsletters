## The Pain Paradox: How Fear of Pain Creates More Pain

Pain isn’t in your tissues, but in your brain. A Netherlands study found that people with chronic lower back pain who scored high on *pain-related fear* were much more likely to still be in pain six months later, regardless of how bad their pain was initially or how long they’d had it.

What can we learn from chronic pain recoveries about our relationship to our own negative feelings? What might it look like to view pain with curiosity instead of fear?
