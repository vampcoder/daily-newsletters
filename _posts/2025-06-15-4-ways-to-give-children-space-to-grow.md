# 4 Ways to Give Children Space to Grow

Labels stick tenaciously, even when they’re inaccurate. A child with an identity of "troublemaker", "slow learner" or even "gifted" can shape how they perceive themselves and how others treat them, often becoming self-fulfilling prophecies.

Here are four ways we can avoid labelling children prematurely, allowing them the space and freedom to evolve into their authentic selves.
