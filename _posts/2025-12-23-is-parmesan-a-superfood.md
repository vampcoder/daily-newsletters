First, a little confession: the reason I look forward to having my veggie starter every day is because I usually hide it under… a MOUNTAIN of parmesan. I am literally obsessed with parmesan. I genuinely think it’s a superfood... but let me explain why:

*   It has lots of **protein** (10g of protein per 1oz chunk)
*   It’s high in **leucine** (an essential amino acid which has been shown to be key in muscle building and longevity)
*   It’s high in **choline** (not as much as eggs but still a super good source)
*   It’s **low in lactose** if you pick an aged parmesan (>12 months aged)

So this week’s advent calendar recipe is like *cauliflower on steroids*. It's the perfect indulgent (but strategic) veggie starter for your next xmas holiday dinner. And yes, **it still counts as a veggie starter if you add protein or healthy fats** to your veggies! Just keep the bread or other starches/sugars for after, if you can.
