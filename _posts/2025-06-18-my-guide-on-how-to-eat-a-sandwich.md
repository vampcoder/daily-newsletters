Today, let’s tackle one of the most common questions I get:  

***“What do I do when I eat a sandwich?”***  

As you know, one of the best ways to reduce glucose spikes is to **eat your food in the right order**:  

🥦 Fiber first  
🍳 Proteins and fats next  
🍞 Starches and sugars last  

This order works because the **fiber** in the veggies creates a **protective “mesh”** in your intestines, slowing down the absorption of glucose and flattening the glucose curve of your entire meal. It’s one of my favorite hacks because it’s simple and powerful.  

**But what happens when your meal is a sandwich?**  

Do you need to awkwardly open it up, eat the lettuce and tomato first (fiber), then the chicken and cheese (proteins and fats), and then the bread (starch)?  

The answer is NO.  

You don’t need to decompose your sandwich. Instead, here’s the simple hack I use:  

👉 **Add a veggie starter** before your sandwich.  

Before diving into my sandwich here, I had a mixed salad and cauliflower starter.
