**Favorite insight:**

*There are four types of human behavior: Red, Blue, Green, and Yellow. If a four-person team had all four colors:*

* *The Red (high “dominance” trait) would boldly and confidently make decisions.*
* *The Yellow (high “inspiration” trait) would enthusiastically generate ideas and dominate meeting discussions.*
* *The Green (high “stability” trait) would say yes to every request and quietly complete most of the work.*
* *The Blue (high “compliance” trait) would meticulously check the work, ask numerous questions, and smile the least.*

*Approximately 5% of people are just one color. About 80% of people are two colors, one primary color and a splash of another color. The remaining few are a balance of three or four colors. This means we can better understand the behavior of roughly 85% of the people we interact with by identifying their primary colors. When you learn to see people's colors, you'll be less irritated by people's behavior because you can anticipate their tendencies and communicate more effectively with them.*
