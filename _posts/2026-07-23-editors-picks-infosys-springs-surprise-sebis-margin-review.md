# Editor's Picks: Infosys springs surprise, Sebi's margin review

**Date:** July 23, 2026

Infosys has sprung a surprise on the Street with its quarterly performance, while market regulator Sebi has initiated a review of margin rules. Here’s a look at the top stories from today’s edition.

## Infosys springs a surprise

Infosys delivered a better-than-expected set of numbers, sending a positive signal for the IT sector. The company’s performance lifted sentiment, even as analysts look for sustainability in demand and pricing.

## Sebi’s margin review

Sebi is examining the existing margin framework, a move that could have far-reaching implications for traders and market stability. The review is part of the regulator’s ongoing efforts to recalibrate risk management.
