**“Nerves are good. They mean you care.” — Lucy Tops**

How you perform in high-pressure moments...

* important meetings
* competitions
* interviews
* exams

...has a huge impact on your career.

Here's a video and 1-pager on five proven techniques to perform under pressure, drawn from the best performance psychology books. Together, they form the **B.R.A.V.E. method**.

[B.R.A.V.E. Performance Video](https://youtu.be/K4exMXvel4k) (7:30 min)

[1-Page Summary](https://download.filekitcdn.com/d/8UGoEYUY8hNJcBFmksRBVo/udZbcXtgiZNBC6a1wpQqzc)

**Favorite Takeaway:**

*Every intimidating performance is just a series of actions you've done before, but in a different setting:*

* An upcoming exam is just like another practice test.
* Delivering a presentation to a big crowd is like delivering it in your living room to your dog…same words, same gestures.

Before your next performance, complete the following sentence:

***"This is just like ________________________.”***

Fill in the blank with a similar, lower-stakes version of what you're about to do. Say that phrase 3 times while visualizing both scenarios side-by-side. Notice the pressure diminishing as you equate them.
