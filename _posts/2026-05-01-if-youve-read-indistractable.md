Following up on my last note, I want to come back to that gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it.

If you’ve already read *Indistractable*, you’re likely familiar with the ideas. You’ve thought about how you want to spend your time and what tends to pull you away from it.

And still, at times, our follow-through isn’t always there. (My family certainly experiences the same at various points of our days.)

This led us to create *The Indistractable Workbook*, which turns the same concepts into a series of short, practical exercises. It’s designed to be used at your own pace, helping you build the skill of managing your attention before it starts to drift.

If you’ve been meaning to revisit these ideas in a more practical way, this is a simple place to start.
