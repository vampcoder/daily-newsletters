We’ve all given our parents some version of the standard digital safety lecture: *Never share an OTP, and never give your password to a stranger.*

We assume that as long as they follow those rules, their savings are safe.

But our latest case turns that logic on its head.

It involves a 77-year-old retired Chartered Accountant whose family includes a financial consultant and a US-based systems engineer. On paper, they had all the financial and technical literacy you could ask for. Yet, over a single weekend, ₹18 lakhs vanished from their accounts.

There was no psychological trap. No passwords were typed out, and no OTPs were read aloud. Instead, a malicious APK link secretly hijacked the phone's accessibility features, running transactions in the background while the phone sat in a pocket.

When a scam doesn't need you to make an active mistake, basic safety checklists stop working. It leaves us with a much tougher question: How do you protect your wealth against something you can't even see happening?
