**“The curse of a bad set of habits is that all the unnecessary things you’re doing actually come to seem necessary.” – Dan Heath**

In this book summary, you'll learn three proven reset strategies to reverse declining results (sales, work output, or performance ratings) and regain momentum.

**Favorite Takeaway:**

What's one constraint that, if removed, would unleash a flood of progress in an area you're struggling with?

Test your answer by determining if the following statement holds true: I could improve five or more things in this area: _____, but if I fail to improve this one thing: _____, none of those other improvements matter.

One man in the book discovered that the bottleneck in his marriage was gratitude. Without genuine appreciation for his wife, no amount of counseling sessions, vacations, or loving gestures would reignite their connection. After addressing his lack of gratitude, their affection and playfulness returned.

You'll find that most breakthroughs don't occur by working harder, but by zooming out to find the one constraint you need to address.
