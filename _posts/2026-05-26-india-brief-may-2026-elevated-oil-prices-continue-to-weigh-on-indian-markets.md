We are thrilled to launch the May edition of our video series **'India Brief'** - house view on equity and fixed income markets.

## Key Takeaways

- Higher oil prices set to widen Current Account Deficit (CAD) and have also clouded the growth outlook over the next 12 to 18 months.
- We retain our tactical mild underweight stance on Indian equities.
- While valuations have moderated, we believe the weak investor sentiment due to higher oil prices and India's limited exposure to the AI investment cycle are likely to weigh in near-term.
