## Editor's Picks: Tata’s growth bet, IPO fundraising high and Bitchat challenge

**July 27, 2026**

---

### 1. Tata’s growth bet

[Original article link]

Tata Group is making bold investment bets across new sectors, aiming for robust growth in the next decade. The strategy is to leverage its scale and brand strength to dominate emerging markets, even as current economic conditions remain uncertain.

### 2. IPO fundraising high

[Original article link]

Indian IPO markets are witnessing a historic boom, with fundraising at record levels. Companies across sectors are rushing to list, backed by strong domestic and institutional investor interest. This trend signals confidence in the economy, but also raises questions about valuation sustainability.

### 3. Bitchat challenge

[Original article link]

The new social messaging app Bitchat is rapidly gaining users, posing a direct challenge to established players. Its unique features and privacy-focused approach have captured attention, forcing incumbents to respond. The coming months will determine whether Bitchat can sustain its momentum and become a lasting competitor.
