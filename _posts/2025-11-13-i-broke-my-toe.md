I broke my toe the other day. A lovely spiral fracture from walking straight into my coffee table.

And as I was climbing two flights of stairs yesterday (which seemed like an *impossible* mission), I had a flashback about how that felt like ***climbing Everest*** when I was pregnant – out of breath within 30 secs.

And then I also remembered how nauseous and hangry I was at the beginning of my pregnancy. My hormones completely out of whack, a “good” breakfast feeling perfectly fine one day and leaving me starving the next day.

At the time, all I wanted was to **know exactly what was happening in my body**: what could I do to feel better? all while giving the best possible environment for my baby as well? I wanted to **take back some control**.

**My first point-of-call**: asking friends and family, people who’ve experienced all this before, multiple times for some. But I was sorely disappointed... most of them just told me *“yeah, that’s normal, pregnancy is just rough. But don’t worry your baby will get what it needs from you.”*

**My second point-of-call**: my doctor. And again, disappointment, same response: “*it’s normal to have ups and downs, your baby will get what it needs from you*.”
