---

## Is to categorise happiness, to compare it, to scare it away?

The question haunts the act of curation. When we label an experience, rank it against another, or slot it into a taxonomy of feelings, we risk chasing away the very thing we hoped to hold. Happiness, like a startled bird, takes flight at the approach of the net.

This week, we wander through the strange corners of the internet where poetry meets merchandise, where Wikipedia's gaps reveal more than they hide, and where time becomes a thing to be pondered rather than measured. Each stop is an invitation to slow down and feel, not merely to know.

---

### Poetry Merch

Somewhere between the serious literary journal and the gift shop, poetry has found a new home: on t-shirts, tote bags, and coffee mugs. Is this a dilution of the art form or a democratization? Perhaps both. When a line from a beloved poem appears on a hoodie, it becomes a wearable reminder—not of the poem's full depth, but of its capacity to accompany us through the day. It is not the poem itself, but a small talisman of its resonance.

And yet, we must ask: does commodification cheapen the magic? Or does it simply allow more people to hold a piece of it close?

---

### Wikipedia Holes

Every encyclopedia has its lacunae, but Wikipedia's gaps are often as telling as its entries. Scan the pages and you'll notice what is missing: the under-documented, the overlooked, the unsung. These holes are not accidents—they are mirrors of our collective attention. Where we fail to write, we fail to remember. The call to fill these gaps is not just an editorial task; it is an act of cultural preservation.

---

### A Contemplation of Time

Time is the strangest of currencies—we spend it, lose it, save it, and yet never truly own it. The Weekender often circles back to this theme, not to solve it, but to sit with it. Perhaps time is not a river but a spiral, returning us to the same questions with new eyes. To contemplate time is to contemplate ourselves.

---

Until next time, hold your happiness loosely and let it stay.
