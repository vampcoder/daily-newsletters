## Editor's Picks

### Reliance Industries beats Street expectations

Reliance Industries has reported quarterly earnings that surpassed analyst forecasts, driven by strong performance across its refining, petrochemical, and retail segments. The conglomerate's digital services arm also contributed to the upbeat numbers.

### Zepto's valuation cut

The quick-commerce unicorn Zepto has seen its valuation slashed in a recent fundraise, reflecting a more cautious investor sentiment towards high-burn startups. Despite the markdown, the company continues to expand its operations and delivery network.

### UPI fee plan

The government is considering a plan to introduce a nominal fee on UPI transactions to help payment service providers recover costs. The proposal has sparked debate over its potential impact on digital payment adoption and the profitability of fintech firms.
