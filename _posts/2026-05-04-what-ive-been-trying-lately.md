A simple question helped me stay on track

I’ve been thinking about the replies I’ve received over the past few days. Many of you described something I recognize in my own life as well: having a clear idea of what you want to do, and then finding yourself doing something else when the moment comes.

One small thing I’ve been trying is to pause when I feel the urge to switch tasks, and ask:

***What am I feeling right now?***

Sometimes it’s subtle: a sense of restlessness, a bit of fatigue, a dash of boredom. Even noticing it seems to create a little space to decide what to do next.
