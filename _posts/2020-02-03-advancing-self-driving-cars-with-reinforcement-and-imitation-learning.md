How machines could handle the infamous unprotected left turn like a human, and more

Hello! My name is Oliver, and I’m the CEO of Voyage. My days are spent building a car that can drive itself, and serving the people and communities who need this technology most.

Given the considerable societal and safety implications of robotic cars, historically built behind a cloak of secrecy, I am starting Let Me Level 5 With You in an effort to shine a light on all things self-driving cars (and because I love puns). I do so with the hope that this transparency promotes greater trust in this life-saving technology, and that we can accelerate the rollout of safe self-driving cars across the world.

Let’s begin!
