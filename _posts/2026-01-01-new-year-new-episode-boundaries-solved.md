A lot of people think setting boundaries is just about saying "no" or standing up for yourself in a dramatic moment. But real boundaries aren't about conflict or control—they're about clarity, intention, and long-term well-being.

In this episode, Drew and I dive into what healthy boundaries actually look like: the small, consistent choices that help you protect your energy, communicate your needs, and build relationships rooted in mutual respect. We break down the science of boundaries, like why they're so hard to set and what makes them stick. And of course, we offer practical tools you can start using right away.
