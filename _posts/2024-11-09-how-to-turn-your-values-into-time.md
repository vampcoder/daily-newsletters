**The Illusion of Control Bias**

The illusion of control bias, a term first coined by psychologist Ellen Langer, is a tendency for people to believe they have more control over outcomes than they actually do. Yet while those actions might give us a momentary sense of control, they're often just distractions that pull us away from our real goals. How do we outsmart this bias?
