The Indian SaaS market is expected to grow to $50 billion by 2030! The future of software as a service (SaaS) seems exciting globally, and more so in India. In a study by Bessemer Venture Partners, it is estimated that the Indian SaaS market is expected to grow from $13 billion in 2022 to $50 billion by 2030.

So, what’s stopping an Indian company from becoming the next Salesforce?

In the latest episode of the Create Wealth podcast, we explore exactly this. I speak with Kuldeep Dhankar, founder of Last9.io, one of India’s leading SaaS companies. With over 20 years of experience across Nokia, Microsoft, and CleverTap, his journey building brands and solving problems for businesses is a masterclass for those who work in SaaS businesses and sales roles across industries.

This is the longest episode of our podcast, and with good reason. Kuldeep’s deep knowledge of emerging technologies, the SaaS segment in India, and his intensive insights from global tech companies make this episode a treasure trove of experiences and learnings.

In this episode, you'll learn:
1. Why India is a goldmine for SaaS companies
2. How to build a winning SaaS product from the ground up
3. The secret sauce to scaling your SaaS sales
4. How VCs see the booming SaaS landscape in India

Plus, Kuldeep shares his insights on:
- Building a successful sales team
- Branding and sales lessons from Shah Rukh Khan
- The true meaning of wealth and navigating the world of wealth managers

My favourite segment? The part where Kuldeep shares his views on creating value and what makes a good salesperson.

“A good salesperson is one who is inquisitive and genuinely interested in knowing and understanding the person on the other side. You will never sell unless you create value for your buyer.”
