What’s stopping doctors from becoming major wealth creators?

In the past 20 years, managing the wealth of India’s wealthiest families, I have had the privilege of advising multiple doctors and while I have always been awed by the depth of their knowledge and passion to serve patients, the challenges they face in creating and managing wealth are obvious.

The unique earning lifecycle, time constraints and lack of formal financial education leave doctors lagging in their wealth-creation journeys.

So, how can doctors run successful practices and become major wealth creators?

In the latest episode of the Create Wealth podcast I speak to **Shashank ND**, CEO and founder of [Practo](https://practo.com).
