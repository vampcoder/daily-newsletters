Picture this: It's 2007. Everyone in the travel industry is chasing the "cool" segments - flights, hotels, premium travel. But not the founders of ixigo. Aloke and Rajnish decided to focus on the railway traveller, an underserved and often ignored segment. Their obsession with solving pain points like checking PNR status and providing real-time train information turned ixigo into a ₹6,000 crore company. In this conversation, the co-founders share how they built a market leader by betting on an unlikely niche and relentlessly improving the customer experience.
