Chen](https://open.substack.com/users/42713285-viv-chen?utm_source=mentions) has built a thriving fashion community in a city not known for its sartorial sensibilities: San Francisco. Her Substack, [The Molehill](https://open.substack.com/pub/themolehill?utm_source=mentions), explores style through a distinctly local lens: [What does your Berkeley Bowl grocery run outfit reveal about you](https://substack.com/redirect/6561e8fb-e5d6-4594-8b7d-eaf2edd2561b?j=eyJ1IjoiM3ZldWEifQ.GVk46KgTB2RDl8XiyIYsnzgCOJGT_g7PXHdsRNh4piw)? Which sweater works best when you’re waiting for a [Hella Wet Levain loaf from Acme on a deceptively sunny-but-breezy day](https://substack.com/redirect/0f1739af-8191-409a-bc6a-bb72ca3dd33f?j=eyJ1IjoiM3ZldWEifQ.GVk46KgTB2RDl8XiyIYsnzgCOJGT_g7PXHdsRNh4piw)? We joined Viv aboard a classic cable car to bum around the city and discuss people-watching, fashion’s socio-political dimensions, and her readers’ hyper-niche fashion questions.  This film is the latest in our Substack on Film series, directed and shot by [Nathan Zack](https://open.substack.com/users/17572061-nathan-zack?utm_source=mentions) in 16mm.
