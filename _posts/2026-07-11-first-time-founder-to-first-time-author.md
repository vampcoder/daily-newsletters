Two and a half years ago, I started writing ***The Millionaire Employee***. Today, I finally get to share it with you.

For 20 years, I've watched people work hard for their equity, only to make some of their biggest financial decisions with very little guidance. The information is out there, but it's scattered across Reddit threads, podcasts, blogs, etc. The full picture has always lived in small circles and conversations most people are never part of. I wanted to change that.

And that's how the idea for ***The Millionaire Employee*** was born.

I wanted to bring everything an employee in India needs to know about ESOPs into one place. This book does exactly that, following the complete journey from the first time you see ESOPs in your offer letter to what you do when the money finally arrives in your account.

If you hold ESOPs, or know someone who does, I hope this finds its way to you.
