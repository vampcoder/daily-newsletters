I know how easy it is to let an hour or two slip away to distractions, whether watching the news, constantly checking email, or scrolling on social media.

Over a week, that’s at least seven hours lost, likely a full workday. And before I wrote *Indistractable*, I wasted far more time than that.

That doesn’t mean there’s something wrong with you—distraction isn’t a personal failing. In fact, we live in a world intentionally designed to pull our attention away.

But with the right system, you can reclaim that time and devote it to what truly matters instead.
