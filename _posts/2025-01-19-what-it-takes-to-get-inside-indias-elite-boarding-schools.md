This is where India's future leaders and wealth creators are moulding their futures.

A conversation with one of our relationship managers last week turned to something fundamental to wealth creation - the value of consistency over perfection. As we discussed what makes people consistent, we arrived at a simple truth: discipline is the foundation.

But this led us to a question that stayed with me: Is discipline something we can develop as adults, or does it need to be instilled early in life?

His response came from an interesting place. As an alumnus of Mayo College, Ajmer - established in 1875 by the Earl of Mayo - he shared how boarding schools have refined the art of building discipline over centuries. Each day followed a precise rhythm, from dawn to dusk, creating patterns that shaped young minds.

'We didn't just learn discipline,' he reflected, 'we lived it. It wasn't a separate lesson - it was simply how life worked there.'

This glimpse into one of India's oldest boarding schools opened up a fascinating window into a world that has shaped generations of leaders, industrialists, and changemakers. As India's wealth grows and more families seek the best education for their children, these historic institutions - and the newer ones joining their ranks - offer interesting insights into how education at the highest level is evolving in our country.
