## Joan Didion, Movie Audience Critic

[The Bulwark's latest essay](https://open.substack.com/pub/thebulwark/p/joan-didion-movie-audience-critic) explores how Joan Didion, known for her incisive prose and cultural commentary, also brought a unique perspective to film criticism. Her reviews delved beyond plot and performance to examine the audience's role in shaping a film's meaning—and what our reactions say about us.

This piece invites readers to consider how entertainment reflects and influences societal values, and why the act of watching is never passive. It's a reminder that criticism can be a form of self-reflection, offering a mirror to both the screen and the viewer.
