Most of the beliefs shaping your decisions were never yours to begin with. They were planted by parents, teachers, and a culture you may have outgrown years ago.

It’s something I keep coming back to in *Beyond Belief*, and my friend Steve Kamb lived it. He built Nerd Fitness into one of the biggest fitness communities online, then spent five years forcing himself to become a manager because he believed that’s what CEOs do. He was miserable.

When he finally asked, “Is this belief really true?” he quit managing, handed the company to someone who actually wanted the job, and went back to writing. His new book, *How to Try Again*, grew out of that pivot.

In this article, Steve walks you through how to find the beliefs you never chose and what to do once you spot them.

Steve goes back to where our beliefs actually come from—like how a World War I propaganda campaign still shapes how we eat, and the identity labels our parents stuck on us as kids still shape how we behave. If you’ve ever caught yourself thinking “that’s just who I am,” start here.
