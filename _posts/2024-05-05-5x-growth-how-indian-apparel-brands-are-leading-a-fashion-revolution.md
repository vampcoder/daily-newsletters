## The Rise of D2C Online Apparel Brands in India

The Indian fashion landscape is witnessing exciting transformation and growth, driven largely by the rise of direct-to-consumer (D2C) online fashion brands.

The online fashion and lifestyle market in India has reached an impressive USD 11 billion and is on a trajectory to grow to approximately USD 35 billion by 2028, expanding at a 25% compound annual growth rate (CAGR), as revealed by a joint report from Bain & Company and Aditya Birla Group's TMRW.

As traditional players like Louis Philippe and Puma adapt to the digital wave, their online revenues have grown by 34% since 2019. Meanwhile, new-age brands such as The Souled Store and Bewakoof have expanded their online presence by 33%, with projections suggesting an annual growth rate of 35%. Those brands exceeding INR 250 crore in revenue are expected to increase revenue 5x by FY28.

## What Is Triggering the Growth of D2C Apparel Brands?

While online shopping has become a craze across generations, the rise of digital disruptors in apparel is being fueled by Gen Z and millennials.

Here's a visual representation of the data concerning the impact of digital disruptors on different consumer groups:

- **Traffic distribution to digital disruptors**: Gen Z and Millennials dominate traffic, with 35% and 45% respectively, leaving only 20% to other age groups.
- **Current spending**: Younger consumers (18-to-24-year-olds) account for 24% of spending, a significant portion compared to just 13% from those 45 and older.
- **Projected spending by FY28**: By FY28, about 75% of spending on digital disruptor brands will come from consumers under the age of 45.
