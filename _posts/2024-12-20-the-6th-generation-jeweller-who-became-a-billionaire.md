In the latest episode of the Create Wealth podcast, I talk to Saurabh Gadgil - the 6th generation of the Gadgil family. Chairman and Managing Director of PNG Jewellers, over the last 25 years, Saurabh has been at the helm of Maharashtra’s most beloved jewellery brand.

In Pune, PNG Jewellers isn’t just a household name—it’s a tradition. From its early days as the most trusted jeweller for lakhs of families in Maharashtra, PNG has come a long way. Today, it is a listed company with a market capitalisation of over INR 10,000 crore and over 40 stores in Maharashtra.

My conversation with Saurabh in his beautiful home was a sneak peek into the billionaire club! How they think, grow their business and create wealth.

What struck me most was how Saurabh transformed his family's legacy: from introducing SWOT analysis to a business that began in the 1800s to building sophisticated data analytics for predicting customer trends. All while preserving the core values that helped his ancestors cater to multiple generations and build unshakeable customer trust.

In this episode of the Create Wealth Podcast, Saurabh shares:

- The journey from street-side gold trading to an INR 10,000 crore market cap company
- Their unique "quick commerce" model that outperforms peers in ROI
- How data-driven merchandising helps them operate with 50% less inventory than competitors
- The strategy behind choosing brand ambassadors like Madhuri Dixit, Salman Khan and Sushmita Sen.
