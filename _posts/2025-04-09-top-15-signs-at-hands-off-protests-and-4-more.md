## Top 15 Signs at Hands Off Protests

The Hands Off protests have drawn massive crowds, and as always, the signs are a major part of the action. Here are the top 15 signs that stood out:

1. **"Hands Off Our Democracy"** – a classic, but still hits hard.
2. **"Veterans for Democracy"** – service members standing up for the rule of law.
3. **"I Miss the Constitution"** – simple, sad, and to the point.
4. **"Protect Our Freedoms"** – a broad call to anyone paying attention.
5. **"No Kings in America"** – direct and defiant.
6. **"We Voted for Rights, Not Rollbacks"** – a reminder of what's at stake.
7. **"Healthcare is a Human Right"** – a favorite at any march.
8. **"Don't Mess With Medicare"** – seniors and allies speaking out.
9. **"Science, Not Censorship"** – experts pushing back.
10. **"Funding Cuts Hurt Our Kids"** – parents and teachers on the front lines.
11. **"Get Your Hands Off Our Schools"** – education under attack.
12. **"Protect Voting Rights"** – the bedrock of democracy.
13. **"Deport Hate, Not People"** – a quick-witted jab at immigration policy.
14. **"We Are the Majority"** – solidarity in numbers.
15. **"Democracy Dies with Silence"** – a call to keep showing up.

The posters capture the mood of the moment: angry, witty, and very, very loud.
