**TEST** Montana Turns Patients Into the "AI Trial"

**👀 What's happening:** This week Montana became the first US state to launch an AI-driven clinical trial, effectively turning patients into "AI trials." The initiative marks a significant step in the use of artificial intelligence in healthcare, with AI algorithms overseeing patient data and treatment protocols in real-time. This could drastically speed up medical research and personalize patient care, but it also raises important questions about consent and oversight.

Virus Design Crosses a New Line

In a related development, researchers have used AI to design a virus that evades natural immunity, a breakthrough that could have both beneficial and dangerous applications. This new capability pushes the boundaries of bioengineering and reinforces the need for robust safety measures.

Google Owns None of the Business

Meanwhile, Google's latest AI ventures reveal a business model where the company provides the AI infrastructure but does not own the businesses that use it. This approach could redefine how tech giants engage with startups and traditional industries, echoing the way Google's Android powers phones without owning them.
