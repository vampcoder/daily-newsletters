## Understanding the unlisted market

While most of us are familiar with listed equities, pre-IPO company shares are actually unlisted shares that are expected to be listed soon. These are equity shares of companies not listed on any stock exchange and, therefore, do not trade publicly. Such shares have become the new investment avenue everyone is talking about.

To understand them better, let's compare listed and unlisted equities. When it comes to listed shares, investors can access them through Initial Public Offerings (IPOs), Follow-on Public Offers (FPOs) and buying/selling on the stock exchanges. Unlisted shares are usually bought and sold directly between individuals or through intermediaries, such as brokers, dealers, or direct sellers.
