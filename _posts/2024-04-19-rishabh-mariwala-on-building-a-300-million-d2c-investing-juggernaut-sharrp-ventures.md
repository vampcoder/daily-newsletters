Imagine being the heir to one of India's biggest FMCG empires. Most would play it safe, enjoying the family riches. But not Rishabh Mariwala! This bold maverick took the road less traveled - transforming Sharrp Ventures from a $10M family office into a $300M D2C TITAN!

His unconventional moves? Shifting from 90% public equity to over 40% in unlisted private companies, racking up 35X returns on Nykaa and 50X on Mamaearth, and proving patient family office capital can dominate high-growth D2C better than VCs.

In the latest exclusive podcast, Rishabh shares insights from his playbook:

- When is the ideal inflection point to launch a family office (clue: it's not just about having enough money)
- How Sharrp's private investments help fuel innovation in Marico's core FMCG business
- His asset allocation framework for balancing public market vs private investment exposure
- His radical evaluation criteria for identifying D2C blockbusters to back at the ground floor
- Why brand building is a marathon requiring patient long-term capital (and how VCs often fail here)
- The costliest mistakes he sees D2C founders make that cripple their growth potential

Plus unfiltered wisdom gems like:

- "A tree can't grow under a banyan" - His brutally honest take on the unique struggles next-gen entrepreneurs face
- "Patient capital, not VC mindset" - How family offices with their long-term view can beat VCs at their own game
