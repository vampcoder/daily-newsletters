I eat **3 to 4 eggs** *every single day*. But almost every time I mention this, someone says: “Wait… aren’t eggs bad for your heart?”

For years, we were told that eggs were dangerous. Too much cholesterol. Bad for your heart. Doctors said limit them. Guidelines warned against them. Egg whites only — remember that?

Well, today I’m taking you **on a journey through that story**, and explaining why it’s time to **let go of the fear** and reclaim the egg as a health superfood.

## Why 10 Eggs a Day is Fine: The Science of Cholesterol & Heart Health
