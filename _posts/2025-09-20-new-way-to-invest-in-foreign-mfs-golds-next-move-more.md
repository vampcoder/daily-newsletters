## Invest In Foreign Mutual Funds Via GIFT City (Without Restrictions)

GIFT City provides a new avenue for investing in foreign mutual funds, bypassing the usual restrictions. This opens up global diversification opportunities for Indian investors.

## Why Profits Vary So Much Across Industries

HDFC AMC makes a ₹70 net profit from ₹100 sales. Bharti Airtel makes ₹21.6, Reliance just ₹7.5, and Zomato only ₹2.6. Such a huge gap exists because of differences in Gross, Operating, and Net margins. Understanding these metrics reveals how profits really flow across industries.

Let’s break down how margins work:

- **Gross Margin**: Revenue minus cost of goods sold.
- **Operating Margin**: Gross profit minus operating expenses.
- **Net Margin**: Operating profit minus taxes and interest.

Each industry has a distinct margin structure, which explains why asset managers are highly profitable while food delivery platforms operate on razor-thin margins.
