If you hail from Delhi or Uttar Pradesh you have definitely heard of the legendary Goldiee Masala.

With 17000+ distributors, 4000+ team members and 100 team members who have stayed in the company for more than 35 years, in the last 44 years, Goldiee Masala has risen from a local favorite to a top FMCG brand – all while staying true to its Tier 2 roots.

In the latest episode of the Create Wealth podcast with Sandeep Jethwani, we speak to Sudeep Goenka, Director of Goldiee Group, about their remarkable journey.
