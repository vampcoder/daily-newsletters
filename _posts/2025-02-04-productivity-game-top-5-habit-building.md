**Favorite Insight:**

Instead of focusing on an entire routine, focus on the minimum number of steps needed to reach the point of no return—the moment when continuing is more likely than quitting. You’ll typically reach this moment within two minutes of starting any new habit. For example, if you take two minutes to put on gym shorts, clip into your Peloton shoes, and sit on the bike, you’ll almost certainly complete your workout. Therefore, if you want a new habit to stick, script out the exact steps needed to reach the point of unstoppable momentum and then experiment with making it shorter and shorter.
