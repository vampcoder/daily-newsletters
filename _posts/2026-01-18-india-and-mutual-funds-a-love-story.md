# India and Mutual Funds: A love story

Mutual Funds have emerged as the best way for equity market participation.

What AMFI did with *Mutual Fund Sahi Hai* is nothing short of a revolution. The initiative transformed mutual funds from a niche financial product into a household term, making them the go-to choice for millions of Indian investors seeking to participate in the equity market.
