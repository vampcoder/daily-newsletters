I often hear doctors say: “*If you don’t have diabetes, you don’t need to worry about glucose.*” But to me, that’s like saying: “*If you don’t have cavities, don’t bother brushing your teeth.*”

## Why You Should Improve Your Glucose Levels (Even If You Don’t Have Diabetes)

In my brand-new (and last of the season) YouTube episode, I dive deep into the science that shows why glucose levels matter for all of us. Even if your doctor says your numbers are “normal,” studies prove that your fasting glucose and daily spikes can shape everything from your energy, cravings, and mood… to your long-term risk of heart disease, Alzheimer’s, and more.

Here’s what I'm talking about in this episode:

- The *real* healthy fasting glucose range (hint: it’s not just under 100 mg/dL)
- Why prevention matters long before diabetes
- How glucose spikes impact cravings, menopause symptoms, immunity, and mental health
- My favorite hacks + tools to keep glucose steady
