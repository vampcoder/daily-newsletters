# AI to Interpret Your Mind

What if a machine learning model could interpret what’s going on inside our minds?

Recent breakthroughs in AI and neuroscience have brought us closer to this reality. Researchers are developing models that can decode brain activity from fMRI scans or EEG signals, translating thoughts into text, images, or even speech. These technologies hold promise for restoring communication in paralyzed patients, advancing mental health diagnostics, and enhancing brain-computer interfaces.

However, as with any technology that grants access to our inner thoughts, there are profound ethical questions about consent, privacy, and identity. Ensuring that these tools are developed responsibly will be crucial as they move from the lab to real-world applications.
