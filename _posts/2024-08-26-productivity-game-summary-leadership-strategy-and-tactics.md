## Leadership Strategy and Tactics by Jocko Willink

In the following book summary, you'll learn the 4Ds that will make you an extraordinary leader (strategies and tactics from a retired Navy SEAL Commander).

**Favorite insight:**

*Most leaders' instinct is to teach people lessons by making them feel guilty for mistakes, like a parent yelling at a child.*

*However, the fastest way to get people to do the right thing consistently is to immediately accept fault as a leader by saying, *"I let this happen. Now, what can we do to make sure it doesn't happen again?"**

*For example, if someone fails to complete a task on time, say, *"I didn't explain the task well enough. What can we do to ensure that you better understand the requirements next time?"**

*This approach of **extreme ownership** has three powerful effects:*

* *It immediately lowers tension between you and the person you're leading, as they have no need to defend themselves.*
