## How to Actually Enjoy the Holidays 🎄

**The one gift you don’t need?** Drama.

This holiday season, give yourself the permission to step away from the chaos. The best way to enjoy the holidays isn’t buying more gifts or attending every party—it’s about protecting your peace.

So here are two festive rules to think about:

1. **The first rule of dealing with drama:** Don’t hang around people who are constantly creating drama.
2. **The second rule:** Never forget the first one.

That’s it. Simple, but not easy. But if you can master these two rules, you’ll find that the holidays become a lot more joyful—and a lot less stressful.
