# The Founder Thesis Podcast | Learn from disruptive founders

## Raj N's Multi-Decade Journey to IPO: Fintech Wisdom from Zaggle

In this episode of The Founder Thesis Podcast, we dive deep into the fintech world with Raj N, co-founder of Zaggle. Raj shares his incredible multi-decade journey—from the early days of entrepreneurship to taking his company public.

Raj discusses the pivotal moments that shaped Zaggle, the challenges of navigating the complex fintech landscape in India, and the strategic decisions that led to its successful IPO. He offers candid advice for aspiring founders on resilience, leadership, and the importance of building a sustainable business model.

Whether you're an entrepreneur in fintech or simply interested in what it takes to build and scale a company, this conversation is packed with actionable insights and inspiring stories.
