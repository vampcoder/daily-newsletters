# Substack on Film

Substack is proud to present a new series celebrating the writers and creators who make our platform vibrant. Each episode offers an intimate look at the people behind the words, their creative processes, and the stories that drive their work.

## Watch the First Episode

In our debut film, we sit down with Mac and Jon to explore their journey, their inspirations, and the craft of writing in the digital age.

Stay tuned for more episodes and discover the diverse voices shaping the future of publishing.
