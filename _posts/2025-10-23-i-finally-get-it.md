My mornings are so rushed lately, Amol. It mostly goes like: open an eye, feed and change baby, splash water on face, grab some clothes, make coffee, eat something, get to work. Most days I actually take the time to make eggs, but yesterday was a typical example of *no-time-rush-out-the-door-kind-of-day.*

And this is where it usually goes awry. **For me, not enough protein at breakfast = cravings, hunger, and exhaustion by 11am.**

I also recently discovered super interesting studies on **how eating enough protein *at every meal*** (including breakfast) is crucial for your muscles and energy.

**QUESTION:** What do you think of first when I say “breakfast”? Okay okay, you might be biased since you hear me talk about *savoury* *breakfasts* all day every day... but for a lot of people, the answer is ***“cereal”***. Which means: starch and sugar, *with very little protein*. Because the current food environment today is **made to fill us up**, but *not* to fuel our bodies. In fact, studies show that most people consume **3 times less protein at breakfast** (average 13g of protein) compared to dinner (average 38g).
