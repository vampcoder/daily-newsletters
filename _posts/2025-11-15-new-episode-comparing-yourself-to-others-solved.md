Why do we feel like everyone else is doing better than we are—even when we’re objectively doing fine?

Why can one friend’s success inspire you… and another’s quietly destroy your confidence?

These are great questions, and ones that Drew and I unpack in the latest episode of *Solved*. In it, we explore the psychology of comparison—where it comes from, why it’s so hard to escape, and how to stop it from quietly sabotaging your friendships, goals, and self-worth.

You’ll also learn about the upside of comparison, and how to use it to your advantage as a tool for greater self-awareness and growth instead of just a demoralizing bummer. It might even be the secret motivational hack you’ve been looking for all along.
