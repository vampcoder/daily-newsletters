# Quit or Stay: The 50/50 Rule

> "Success does not lie in sticking to things. It lies in picking the right thing to stick to and quitting the rest." — Annie Duke

In this summary, you'll learn why most people quit too late, and the smart framework for **knowing exactly when it's time to walk away.**

## Favorite Takeaway

*What felt like a 50/50 decision was really more like an 80/20 decision in favor of quitting. It just didn't feel that way emotionally.*

*Our internal quit-stay scale has been distorted by sunk cost bias and the fear of being labeled a quitter.*

*Therefore, we must discount the upside of staying and fall back on this helpful heuristic:*

> ***If a quit-or-stay decision FEELS 50/50, it's time to quit.***
