“It's not stress that kills us, it is our reaction to it.” — Hans Selye

In the following steps, you'll learn how to quickly respond to excess stress and find the stress sweet spot.

## Top 5 Steps to Rapidly Reduce Stress

1. **Recognize the onset.** Notice when stress begins to cloud your thinking.
2. **Pause and breathe.** Take three slow, deep breaths to anchor yourself.
3. **Shift perspective.** Ask: “What would 90-year-old me give to have this problem?”
4. **Reframe the urgency.** Realize that today’s deadlines are a sign of a capable, active mind.
5. **Act with clarity.** Choose one small step to move forward, not to eliminate stress but to align with your values.

## Favorite Insight

When you look back at today’s problems from the perspective of your much more frail future self, you realize how lucky you are to have the problems you have.

- If you’re stressed by a work deadline, remember that 90-year-old-you would be thrilled to have a sharp mind capable of meeting deadlines.
- When you’re in a heated relationship conflict, realize that elderly-you would give anything to have the energy and opportunity to make up with someone they love.

Therefore, when anxiety grips you, ask: "What would 90-year-old me give to have this problem?"
