India’s wealthiest are losing trust in bank lockers for storing their gold. They’re choosing a smarter alternative: vaults built right inside gated communities.

In this week’s *Create Wealth* episode, I talk to Vijay Arisetty, ex-Air Force pilot and founder of Aurm, who’s fixing a problem most of us have faced: owning and accessing a locker for our gold and valuables.

Why this matters:

- India holds **₹25,000 tonnes of gold** at home — worth ₹160 lakh crore
- Only **15% of Indians** have a locker in their city
- Banks only insure **₹2–3 lakh** — if theft is proven
- Home safes are vulnerable — some can be **opened** with a ₹3,000 gas cutter

Aurm’s solution? Vaults just 2 minutes away, monitored by **2,000 sensors**, built inside India’s premium gated communities.

In our conversation we discussed:

→ Why India's wealthy are moving away from traditional bank lockers
→ Why tier-2 city families need 3-4 lockers, not one (generational wealth is different from new money)
→ The hidden costs of "free" bank lockers (forced FDs, insurance products you don't need)
→ How to think about physical asset storage as seriously as you think about your mutual fund portfolio

If you own gold, this episode will make you rethink how you're storing it.
