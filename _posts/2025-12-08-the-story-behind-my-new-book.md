Last year, when I was pregnant with my son, I had a simple question: **is there anything I can do right now, during pregnancy, to help my baby have the strongest possible start in life?**

So I did what I love to do: I dove into hundreds of scientific studies to find the answer. I thought I might find just a supplement to take or something along those lines. Well the scale and power of what I discovered blew my mind. The answer was clear, and shocking, and different than what I had been told: a big YES.

Pregnancy isn't just "*take a prenatal and avoid sushi."* And, our baby doesn't just *"take what he needs from us".* Mothers have far more power than we've been told: **what we eat can shape our baby's brain, body, and lifelong health.**

* The evidence is very solid: thousands of studies in the field, and over 400 of them that I cover in the book
* The impact is profound: the food we eat while pregnant influences our baby's future, shape his brain, program his DNA, determine the strength of his organs, influence the risk of diabetes, mental health, obesity, and more

And yet… **no one tells us that**. This book is everything I wish **every parent, future parent, grandparent, doctor, midwife**, *everyone* could know and scream from the rooftops.
