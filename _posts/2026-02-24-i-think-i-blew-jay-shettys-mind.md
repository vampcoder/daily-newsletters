I recently had an intense and enlightening chat with Jay Shetty on his podcast *On Purpose*.

Jay was so surprised by all the pregnancy science I shared with him from my new book. And as he does so brilliantly, he also guided the conversation into much deeper waters.

We talked about my miscarriage, the grief that followed, and the very personal emotions I’ve carried through that experience. It’s one of the most honest and vulnerable conversations I’ve had in a long time.

The episode is out now if you want to listen on your next post-meal walk or whilst doing the dishes.
