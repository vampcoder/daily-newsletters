A lot of people think philosophy is boring, but our latest episode offers a sharp, straight-talking introduction to one of the most grounded philosophies ever created—one that could change your life if you let it.

In this episode Drew and I unpack essential Stoic ideas like focusing only on what’s within your control, accepting what isn’t, and cultivating real emotional strength in a way that’s accessible and immediately useful. Whether you're dealing with stress, frustration, or everyday chaos, this episode equips you to face it all with more composure and perspective.

You’ll come away with a clear grasp of Stoicism’s core principles and simple ways to practice them in your daily life. It’s not about becoming detached or unfeeling—just more resilient in the face of life’s endless uncertainties.

If you're aiming for less emotional reactivity and more mental clarity in your life, this episode gives you the tools to get there.
