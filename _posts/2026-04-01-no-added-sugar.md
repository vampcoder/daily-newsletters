**THIS is one of the most misleading things the food industry has put on a product.**

Millions of people buy into it every day hoping to make a healthy choice. So today let's unpack what it actually means, why the front of a package is almost *never* where the truth lives, and how to actually pick healthy foods without falling into food marketing tricks.

**#1. The first trick: the health halo effect**

The food industry loves to use the health halo effect to sell products. Companies put a phrase that sounds healthy, like "no added sugar" on the front of the packaging to make you *think* the product is good for you, banking on you not turning it over.

Here's the truth: **"no added sugar" does NOT mean "no sugar."**

It just means the sugar in the product was *already* there in the base ingredients. In a fruit juice or a smoothie, for example, the sugar comes from the fruit itself.

But your body does NOT care about that distinction.

A glucose molecule is a glucose molecule. Whether it came from a fruit that was squeezed into a bottle or from a spoonful of table sugar stirred into your tea, your body absorbs it in the same way.
