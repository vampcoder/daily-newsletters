Wealth creators are no longer meeting at gymkhanas and cafés. So, where are India’s wealthy hanging out now? Welcome to the world of private clubs.

Well done-up interiors, state-of-the-art gyms, exclusive art collections, discreet meeting rooms, theatres for private screenings and trending restaurants with bar takeovers - new age private clubs are all this and more.

Private clubs are fast becoming the new power corridors where India’s wealthy and powerful meet.

To take a peek into this world, in the latest episode of the Create Wealth podcast, I talk to Vivek Narain, the founder of Quorum.
