Let's bust the **3 biggest myths** about pregnancy nutrition.

**MYTH #1**

*"Your baby will always get what he needs from you. You don't have to worry about it."*

This is simply not true. There are many nutrients, for example **choline** or **DHA**, where if you don't eat enough of them during pregnancy, your baby will not get enough of them as he grows in your womb.

Today, about **90%** of pregnant moms don't eat enough choline ([read study](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KKVKYX1ZCQXJRFTSNAWKM5VX_3)), and about **70%** of pregnant moms don't eat enough DHA ([read study](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KKVKYX1ZCQXJRFTSNAWKM5VX_4)).

**MYTH #2**

*"Your placenta will filter things out and only give your baby the good stuff, keeping out all the bad stuff."*

This is also not true. The placenta is not like a great fortress. The placenta is more like **a bouncer outside a bar** who's been told: *"hey, bouncer, as long as there are more people outside the bar than inside the bar, just keep letting people through."* The placenta *trusts* that whatever is in your bloodstream should be mirrored in your baby's bloodstream. So **everything that you eat, your baby also "eats"**.

**MYTH #3**

*"Since you're going to gain weight anyway, you should just eat whatever you want."*

Not true! What you eat during pregnancy has an impact on **your baby's DNA programming** *for life*. It's like sending him little postcards of what the world is going to be like. So his little body forming actually uses what you eat as an *information* to find out **how he should best form himself** and this can lead to lifelong consequences.
