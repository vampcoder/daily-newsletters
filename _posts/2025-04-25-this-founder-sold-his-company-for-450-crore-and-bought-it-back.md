For the latest episode of the Create Wealth podcast, I sat down with Deep Bajaj, the founder of Sirona — India’s leading female hygiene brand.

Deep's journey is one of innovation, astute leadership, and clarity of thought. He shares how he built the brand from the ground up, the challenges he faced in the niche market, and the strategic decisions that led to a successful exit. But the story doesn't end there. In a surprising turn, Deep decided to buy the company back from his investors, and he explains the reasoning and the process behind that bold move.

Listen to the full conversation to hear the insights and lessons from Deep's unique entrepreneurial path.
