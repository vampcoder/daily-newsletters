# This Little-Known Index Fund Can Make You Rich

When people think of index funds, they usually imagine the S&P 500 or the total stock market. But there's a lesser-known fund that combines the best of both worlds: the iShares Exponential Technologies ETF (XT).

The fund tracks the S&P 500 Tech Titans Index, which is a market-cap-weighted index of the 50 largest technology and tech-enabled companies in the S&P 500. This includes giants like Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, and Google, but also up-and-coming names like Nvidia and Tesla.

What makes this fund so compelling is its focus on innovation. Unlike a traditional tech fund that holds software and hardware companies, XT targets companies that are using technology to disrupt their industries—whether it's in healthcare, finance, or energy. This gives you exposure to the entire innovation economy, not just the narrow tech sector.

But the real kicker is the cost. With an expense ratio of just 0.13%, you're getting access to the world's most innovative companies for a fraction of the cost of a typical actively-managed fund. Over time, that fee difference can add up to thousands of dollars in savings.

Of course, no investment is without risk. Tech stocks can be volatile, and the concentration in mega-cap names means you're betting on the continued dominance of a few companies. But if you're looking for long-term growth and you're comfortable with some ups and downs, XT is a smart way to add an innovation tilt to your portfolio.

In a world where you're constantly told to just buy the S&P 500, thinking outside the box—or outside the index—can pay off. This little-known fund might just be the secret to building wealth in the digital age.
