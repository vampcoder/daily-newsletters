> **“Enthusiasm is common. Endurance is rare.” – Angela Duckworth**

In this book summary, you’ll learn four ways to **grow your grit.**

**Favorite insight:**

Angela surveyed 16,000 adult Americans to determine what was a greater contributor to grit: obtaining a feeling of pleasure or a greater sense of purpose. She found that people on the upper half of her grit scale experienced a similar level of pleasure in what they were doing, but she found that higher levels of purpose directly correlated to higher levels of grit. Angela says, “The grittiest people see their ultimate aims as deeply connected to the world beyond themselves.”

You could be a bricklayer simply laying the bricks for the wall of a cathedral, or you could be a bricklayer building the house of God. You get to determine what purpose or greater meaning your work has. Whatever you come up with has the capacity to raise your level of grit.
