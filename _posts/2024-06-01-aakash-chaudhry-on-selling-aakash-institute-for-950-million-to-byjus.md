The $950 million acquisition of Aakash Institute by Byju's was not just a blockbuster deal in the Indian ed-tech industry but, a testament to the phenomenal journey of an institution that has transformed the lives of millions of students and their families.

Founded in 1988 with a single classroom in Delhi, Aakash Institute grew into a behemoth coaching empire with 200+ Centers nationwide, helping 2.5+ lakh aspirants crack the highly competitive medical and engineering entrance exams.
