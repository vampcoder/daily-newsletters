Do you work out? If so, this one's for you.

I did my first hyrox a few weeks ago, and got flooded with questions about **what I eat before and after a workout**. So let's unpack some of this!

**"Do I need to eat before a session?"**

The answer depends entirely on *how hard* you're working, and science shows that the best way to measure that is your heart rate as a percentage of your maximum capacity.

*Warning: there's a bit of math involved! (...I love math)*

To find your max heart rate, you can estimate it by taking 220 and subtracting your age. *(I'm 33, so mine is 187 bpm)* That number is your reference point for everything that follows.

**At 50% max HR: moderate effort**

Walking, a light jog, a gentle gym class.

*For me, this means that my heart rate whilst doing these activities is around 93 bpm (50% of 187 bpm).*

At this intensity, your body burns [mostly fat for fuel](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KS0FHD3TB6Z37J0N5NP2FJ8C_1). No extra glucose needed to perform better.

So you don't actually need to eat before these workouts. A light breakfast focused on protein is totally fine if you prefer, but it won't change your performance much. Do what floats your boat.

**At 85% max HR: high intensity**

Intervals, weightlifting, sprinting

*For me, this means that my heart rate is around 158 bpm (85% of 187 bpm) = me at hyrox!*

Here, your muscles **need** glucose to perform.

Energy utilization vs heart rate

So yes: **eat before**.

But you can give your body the glucose it needs **without creating a big spike**. Just pair your starches with protein and fat.
