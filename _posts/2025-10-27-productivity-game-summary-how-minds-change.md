**“Avoid arguing over a person’s conclusions, and instead work to discover the motivations behind them.” - David McRaney**

In this book summary, you'll learn proven techniques for changing someone's mind. Use these methods to:

* Help a loved one let go of harmful beliefs
* Build support for your ideas at work
* Guide your children to make better decisions

**Favorite Takeaway:**

*Ask someone how well they can explain how a zipper works, and they’ll sound confident because they use one every day. But the moment they try to describe it in detail, they stumble, and their confidence collapses. Psychologists call this the "illusion of explanatory depth": we assume we understand something far better than we actually do.*

***This cognitive quirk applies not only to everyday objects but to deeply held beliefs.***
