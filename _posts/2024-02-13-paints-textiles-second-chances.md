## Paints, Textiles & Second Chances 🙏

**Issue #006** features Shalimar Paints & GHCL Textiles—two companies that are starting a new innings for themselves. We examine their investing thesis and the opportunity they present.

---

### Story 1 of 2 (Reading time: 3 mins)

🎨 **Mera Pyaar Shalimar**

**Shalimar Paints Limited is a has-been in the paints business.**

Just look at these numbers:

- Market share < 1%
- 7 continuous years of losses (FY2017 to FY2023)
- 10Y sales growth = 0%
- Micro cap with ₹1,680 crores market cap
- Old inefficient plants (higher production cost vs competitors)
