"Productivity is meaningless unless you know what your goal is." – Eliyahu Goldratt

In this summary, you'll learn how to **fix a bottleneck in your life** with the F.I.X.E.R. method, and rapidly improve your results.

**Favorite Takeaway:**

The hard part isn't finding your bottleneck. It's leaving your other work alone long enough to fix it. So, this week, pick one task and map it out. Find the slow step, point your effort at it, and ignore the rest until it speeds up.
