Hi Amol,  
I'm typing this with one hand while trying to put my baby to sleep. Story of my life right now!  
And in these moments, I like to reflect on everything my pregnancy (and newborn baby!) taught me. I feel like I learned more in the past year than I have in the 30+ years before it.

As soon as I got pregnant, I started keeping a *(very messy)* notebook: some pages are full of **science**, some are **feelings**, and a lot are tiny **experiments** to make my days feel steadier. I dug deep into the research, and here is what surprised me most: Our diet during pregnancy **shapes our baby's brain, body, and health *FOR LIFE***. We have so much power to build a strong foundation for our baby, and yet there is so much information missing out there. So much we're just not told about.

In my notes, **four key nutrients** kept circling back. Not as rules, not as a diet, but as empowering tools to grow a whole other awesome human being within 9 short months. I am finally ready to share everything I discovered: the **cutting-edge science**, the **shocking facts** I was never told, and the **small things** that *actually* helped.
