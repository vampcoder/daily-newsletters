Hi Amol,

It's Tristan again, and I'm thrilled to share **Reader Public Beta Update #14**. As usual, I'll go over the latest round of major updates to [Reader](https://readwise.io/read). This one includes a huge update to [Readwise](https://readwise.io/) (highlights) too!

I apologize that it's been 8 months since our last update email (though we have posted consistent updates to the [changelog](https://docs.readwise.io/changelog)). As I'm sure most of you know, the software world has changed a lot since the start of 2026, so we've been working overtime to make sense of it all 😛

But we've got much to share, so without further ado, let's get into it! Some of the new features below were foreshadowed in previous updates such as **Global Ghostreader** while others are surprises like **Quick Lookup** and a complete rebuild of the Readwise mobile app.

---

### 🌎 Global Ghostreader (web/desktop)
You can now chat with your *entire library* rather than just a single document. The possibilities here are endless, but the core use is getting an answer grounded in everything you've ever saved with citations linking back to the referenced passages. Ghostreader can even perform meta-tasks like moving documents, adding tags, and so on.

### ✨ Readwise 2.0
We rebuilt the classic Readwise mobile app from the ground up: fresh design, smoother animations, much faster, better search, new ways to capture, and more. This isn't about Reader per se, but it's such a leap, it's worth sharing here.

### 📱 Mobile Chat
Take your Ghostreader conversations on the go—ask questions and get answers from your entire library, right from your phone.

### ⚡ Quick Lookup
Instantly search and jump to any saved highlight or document without leaving your current context.
