The Army declared unlimited tokens through [Ask Sage](https://aisecret.us/r/edb1d82d?m=0e4f1869-00e1-41fa-a8af-66cccb66085e), a platform running Gemini, Llama, and ChatGPT. By mid-June the annual pool of 100 million tokens was dead. Staff got rationing emails. One month from "unlimited" to "please stop." Whether funding survives past October is unknown.  **🌍 How this hits reality:** The Army pushed usage as proof of transformation. Nudge emails for the idle, leaderboards for the eager, automatic top-ups for the hungry. Nobody modeled what "everybody uses AI" actually costs. [A year of budget](https://aisecret.us/r/517a305e?m=0e4f1869-00e1-41fa-a8af-66cccb66085e) lasted thirty days. That's not an adoption success story. That's a procurement office that announced a revolution without reading the price tag.  **🛎️ Key takeaway:** The Army said AI would transform national defense; it couldn't even transform its own budget forecast past June.   |  |  | | --- | --- | | ---  |  | | --- | |  | |   **VALLEY** Dario Steals Twice From the Same House   **👀 What's happening:** Tech blogger Robert Scoble posted that Anthropic was acquiring [Physical Intelligence](https://aisecret.us/r/1cad828f?m=0e4f1869-00e1-41fa-a8af-66cccb66085e), a robotics startup valued at $11 billion.
