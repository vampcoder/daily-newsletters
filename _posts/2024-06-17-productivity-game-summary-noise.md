**“To understand error in judgment, we must understand both bias and noise.” – Daniel Kahneman**

In the following summary, you'll learn how to reduce noise so that people always trust your judgment.

### Favorite Insight

If you truly want to optimize your judgment, use your time before announcing your decision to tap into the wisdom of your "inner crowd." Since mood, fatigue, stress levels, exposure to recent events, and even the weather influence your judgment, you should check in with yourself over the next few hours or days by setting at least three "judgment revision reminders" on your phone.

When you receive the reminder, assume that your previous judgment was off the mark and push yourself as far from your last judgment as possible. Write down this new alternative judgment in a note on your phone, then return to what you were doing. When it's time to announce your final decision, take the average of all the estimates you made.
