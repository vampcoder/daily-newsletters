In my recent newsletters, I’ve explored the exciting developments in India's Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), diving into innovations like Account Aggregators and the Open Credit Enablement Network (OCEN). Today, let’s look at another piece of the digital economy puzzle: data centres — the hidden powerhouses enabling India’s AI revolution.

**Why data centres matter**

Every time you stream a video, use a banking app, or interact with an AI chatbot, your request is processed in a data centre. These facilities house thousands of servers that store, process, and manage vast amounts of data. As AI continues to grow, its need for computing power and storage is exploding, making data centres more crucial than ever.

**India’s data centre boom**

India is fast becoming a global hub for data centres. Factors driving this include:

- **Growing internet penetration and data consumption** – With over 800 million internet users, data demand is skyrocketing.
- **Data localisation policies** – The Indian government encourages storing data within the country.
- **Improving digital infrastructure** – Enhanced connectivity and power supply are making India an attractive destination.

Global tech giants and local players are investing billions in setting up data centres across India, from Mumbai to Chennai to Hyderabad.

**The investment angle**

For investors, the rise of data centres presents a promising opportunity. Real estate trusts (REITs), power and cooling equipment manufacturers, and technology companies are all riding this wave. As AI adoption accelerates, the demand for data centres is expected to grow exponentially.
