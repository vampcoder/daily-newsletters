Hello angels!

I was messaging with a few of you this week and one big topic came up: **cravings.** Here are my top science tips for when you crave candy, chocolate, ice cream, or all of them at once (trust me, I get it.)

**The science:** Eating sweet or starchy foods leads to a glucose spike. A glucose spike is followed by a glucose **drop**. This drop activates the craving centre in our brain ([read the study](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01J96XZ5065ZE44DT0DWMJQS28_2)), making us want to eat more sugar. And the spike and drop start again. We're stuck in a cycle of non-stop cravings, and it's not our fault. We can't override our brain!

***The area of the brain in charge of cravings activates when our glucose drops after a spike.***

So what to do to reduce the glucose spike of a sweet food and avoid the rollercoaster? Keep reading.

**When a craving hits:**

👉 Have something in your stomach. A meal is best, but in a pinch look for protein or fiber: a handful of almonds, baby carrots or another veggie, a greek yogurt.

👉 If you can, have a tablespoon of vinegar (any kind) in a big glass of water.

👉 Eat the food you're craving.

👉 Move your body: a 10 minute walk, cleaning your apartment, dancing to a song.

**Avoid cravings in the first place:**

Remember cravings are not your fault. They're just a consequence of spikes. So, if we reduce spikes from the get-go, we help ourselves.

👉 Savoury breakfast: start your day with a protein-based meal.

👉 Cut out the sugary drinks.

👉 Start your meal with veggies.
