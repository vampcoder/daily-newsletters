The latest episode of *Solved* just dropped: [***How to Build Resilience***](https://solvedpodcast.com/resilience)

A lot of people think resilience means just learning how to “power through” life. But being resilient isn’t about avoiding pain or pretending like hardships don’t exist.

In this episode, Drew and I talk about what resilience really is: the capacity to recover, learn, and grow from hardship. We explore the psychological research that shows how anyone can develop resilience with consistent practice—once they understand and implement the psychological and behavioral tools that build real mental fortitude.

So forget about faking it ‘till you make it and tune in to learn how to approach life’s toughest challenges as invitations to evolve, instead of obstacles to endure.
