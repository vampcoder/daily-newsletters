Medical innovations are extending both lifespan and healthspan. Here's what that means for financial planning ahead.

As science continues to push the boundaries of human longevity, the financial landscape is shifting. Living longer isn't just a personal milestone—it's a financial planning challenge.

Here's what you need to consider:

- **Longer retirement horizons**: With life expectancy rising, your retirement could last 30 years or more. That means your savings need to work harder and last longer.
- **Healthcare costs**: Even with a healthier old age, medical expenses will be a significant part of your budget. Planning for these costs is essential.
- **Wealth accumulation**: The earlier you start saving and investing, the more time your money has to grow, thanks to the power of compounding.

In essence, living longer is a gift, but it requires thoughtful financial preparation to ensure your future is secure.
