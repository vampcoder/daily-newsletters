# The Beliefs That Determine Whether You Succeed With AI

A Stanford study found the least experienced workers gained the most from AI. Here's why.

The assumptions you bring to AI may matter more than the tool itself.

Two colleagues try the same AI tool. One quits after a single mistake, while the other keeps experimenting and finds a breakthrough. The difference is their underlying beliefs, not their intelligence or tech-savviness. Gleb Tsipursky identifies five beliefs that hold people back from getting real value from AI, each backed by peer-reviewed research.
