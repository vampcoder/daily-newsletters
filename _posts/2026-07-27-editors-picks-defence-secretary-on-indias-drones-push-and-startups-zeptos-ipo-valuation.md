The newsletter is dated July 27, 2026, with a focus on the Indian Armed Forces' drone strategy as articulated by Defence Secretary Giridhar Aramane. The editors selected two major stories: Aramane's interview on the military's push for homegrown drones and the role of startups, and Zepto's IPO valuation.

**On India's Drone Ecosystem**
Giridhar Aramane, the Defence Secretary, spoke to Moneycontrol about the armed forces' commitment to purchasing drones from Indian startups. He highlighted that the Ministry of Defence has been actively buying drones from domestic companies, and the government is keen to ensure that the military's requirements are well-defined so startups can deliver effective solutions. Aramane emphasized that the private sector's participation has increased in defence research and development, and the government is working to create a conducive environment for innovation. Key insights from the interview include the need for streamlined procurement processes and clearer technical specifications to help startups scale and meet the armed forces' needs.

**Zepto's IPO and the Quick-Commerce Conundrum**
The second major story examines Zepto's preparations for an initial public offering (IPO) at a potential valuation of $5 billion. The report analyzes the financial sustainability of quick-commerce businesses, noting that while they have grown rapidly, their path to profitability remains a key question for investors. The valuation suggests strong investor confidence, but also brings scrutiny to the sector's high operational costs and competitive dynamics.
