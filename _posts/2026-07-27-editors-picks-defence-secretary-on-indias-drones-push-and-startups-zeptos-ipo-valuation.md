# Editor's Picks: Defence Secretary on India's Drones Push and Startups, Zepto's IPO Valuation

**Date:** July 27, 2026

In this edition, we bring you insights into India's strategic defence modernisation and the latest in the startup IPO boom.

The Defence Secretary discusses India's accelerated push into drone manufacturing, highlighting how startups are now central to the country's defence ecosystem. He emphasises the need for self-reliance, the role of innovation in national security, and the government's commitment to simplifying procurement processes to welcome new players.

Meanwhile, the markets are abuzz with Zepto's IPO valuation, which has captured the imagination of investors. The company's growth trajectory reflects broader trends in quick commerce and the aggressive expansion strategies of Indian startups.

As India navigates these shifts, the convergence of policy support, private enterprise, and capital markets is creating a unique economic moment—one that promises both challenges and opportunities.
