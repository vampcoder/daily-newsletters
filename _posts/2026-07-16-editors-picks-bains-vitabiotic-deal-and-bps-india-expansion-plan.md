# Editor's Picks: Bain’s Vitabiotic deal and BP’s India expansion plan

**Date:** July 16, 2026

Bain Capital is acquiring a significant stake in Vitabiotic, a prominent Indian health supplement brand, in a deal that underscores the growing investor appetite for wellness and nutrition companies in India. The investment is expected to fuel Vitabiotic's expansion amid a surge in health-conscious consumers.

In a separate development, BP is accelerating its expansion in India's fuel retail sector, planning to add thousands of new fuel stations across the country. This move positions BP to compete directly with domestic giants and other international players as India's energy demand continues to rise.

Both transactions highlight India's status as a magnet for global capital, with investors and energy majors alike seeking to tap into the country's long-term growth potential.
