The latest episode of *Solved* just dropped: ***How to Make Friends as an Adult***.

A lot of people think friendship is just about clicking with the right people or having a fun social life. But real friendship isn’t about luck or surface-level connection. It’s about building something meaningful, intentional, and lasting.

In this episode, Drew and I talk about what friendship *really* takes: the ongoing practice of showing up, building trust, and creating shared meaning over time. We explore the research behind why friendship feels harder today—and the practical, science-backed tools anyone can use to make and maintain deeper connections.

So forget the myth that good friendships just happen. Tune in to learn how to create the kind of connection that actually sustains you through every season of life.
