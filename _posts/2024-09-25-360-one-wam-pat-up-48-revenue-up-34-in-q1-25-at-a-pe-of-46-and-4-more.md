# 360 ONE WAM: Q1-25 Results Analysis

360 ONE WAM reported a strong first quarter for fiscal 2025, with profit after tax (PAT) increasing 48% year-over-year and revenue up 34%. The results highlight the company's continued momentum in the wealth and asset management space, despite a premium valuation of 46 times earnings.

The company's ability to grow revenue at a faster clip than costs contributed to the significant bottom-line expansion. With a PE of 46, the market is pricing in sustained high growth, which the company has seemingly delivered so far.

This analysis dives into the key numbers, the drivers behind the growth, and what these metrics mean for long-term investors.
