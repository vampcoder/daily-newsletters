The real truth behind shame and why you can’t just avoid it

In the latest episode of *Solved*, we’re talking all about shame — including its psychological roots, how it functions in our lives, and how to work with it rather than against it.

One way to do that is to be in community with others who are doing the same kind of work. Because real change isn’t only built through learning. It’s also built through action — one small win at a time.
