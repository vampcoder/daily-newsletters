**“At its essence, water flows. It finds its way around (or even through) obstacles. My father (Bruce Lee) would call this having 'no limitations.'”** — Shannon Lee

In this book summary, you’ll learn two ways to adopt the "Be Water" mindset and meet any challenge.

**Favorite insight:**

Imagine leaving a sink running on the top floor of an apartment building—the water doesn't stay contained in your apartment; it seeks out openings in the floor and inevitably finds its way down to the apartment below. To be like water is to embody this relentlessness and persistently find ways to move toward your goals by filling every tiny opening in your day with purpose.

Shannon Lee, Bruce's daughter, once asked her mother if her father ever sat around and did nothing. Her mother's reply was simple, "No." Even during seemingly passive moments, reading a book or watching a boxing match on TV, Bruce was stretching, strengthening, or staying active. If he had to take the stairs, he'd take two at a time. If he had to wait for an elevator, he'd drop and do push-ups.

This is what it means to be like water—to fill the cracks in your time with intention and action, however small.
