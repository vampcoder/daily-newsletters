Cal Newport and I are opening our course, Life of Focus, for a new session.

Life of Focus is a guided, three-month program designed to bring greater focus in your work, life and mind.

**During the first month, we get focused in work**. Deep work is hard, but it’s the key to a rewarding career. After the first month you’ll be getting more done and making progress at what matters most in your profession.

**In the second month, we improve our personal lives**. We’ll cut down on screen time and start making it easier to engage in meaningful pursuits. Whether you want more time for your family, more interesting hobbies or just mental space to relax, we’ll create it together.

**Finally, in the third month, we’ll work on improving how we think and learn**. Focus in your mind is about your capacity to do hard things, and we’ll share science and strategies to keep you sharp.

We know, from working with thousands of students in the program over the last five years, that good intentions aren’t enough. **A more focused life requires changes to our habits, environments and systems.**
