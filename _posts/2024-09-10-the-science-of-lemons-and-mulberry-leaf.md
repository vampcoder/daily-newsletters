In my previous emails you got to learn my food hacks. Now I want to tell you about two more scientific discoveries that can help you reduce your spikes naturally so you feel amazing.

**The science of lemon and glucose:** We have little cells in our digestive system that feel when we’ve eaten food. As soon as they sense food, they release a cool hormone called GLP-1. GLP-1 has a few jobs, like telling us we can stop eating, but also telling our body that glucose is coming – and that it’s time to put any excess glucose from a glucose spike, away. That’s why having a good amount of GLP-1 is very helpful to keep our glucose levels steady.

Scientists have discovered that there is an antioxidant in lemon called eriocitrin (it’s one of the things that give lemon its beautiful yellow colour). When we consume eriocitrin, our little cells produces more GLP-1. So we stay full for longer, reduce cravings, and reduce our glucose spikes. It’s super helpful.

The thing is, you need a lot of eriocitrin to get an impact on your GLP-1 levels. You would have to drink 1.5 liters of lemon juice per day to get enough! Don’t do that. Instead scientists have been able to **extract** eriocitrin and concentrate it, and when you take it every day you can increase GLP-1 by 17% ([study](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01J7E0D1Z7437KM4DK4TD9B41P_1)) AND decrease fasting blood glucose and insulin resistance ([study](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01J7E0D1Z7437KM4DK4TD9B41P_2)).

**The science of white mulberry leaves:** These leaves are incredible: they contain a molecule called DNJ. When we consume DNJ before eating a meal high in carbs (let’s say a pasta dish, a cake, toasted bread, etc…), DNJ connects with our digestive enzymes in charge of breaking down carbs into glucose molecules, and slows them down. As a result, up to 40% of the glucose from that meal never makes it into our bloodstream – but nothing else is blocked. Just some excess glucose. It also reduces our insulin spikes by up to 40% too, which is absolutely crucial to better health. ([study](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01J7E0D1Z7437KM4DK4))
