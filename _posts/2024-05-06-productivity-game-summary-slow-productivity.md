> **“This philosophy rejects busyness, seeing overload as an obstacle to producing results that matter, not a badge of pride.” - Cal Newport**

---

In the following summary, you'll learn how slowing down can lead to more high-quality output over your lifetime.

**Favorite insight:**

*Slowing down gives you space to think and time for your work to evolve into something extraordinary. If your output relies on cognitive effort, your knowledge worker and your success will stem less from how busy you are and how often you discover new ways to get things done. The slow productivity philosophy put forth by Cal Newport promotes good judgment and creative thinking by focusing on three pillars: Do fewer things, Obsess over quality, Work at a natural pace.*
