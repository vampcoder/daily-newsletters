## Maybe You Can Get Tired of All the Winning?

A thought-provoking piece from The Bulwark explores whether the constant string of victories—political, cultural, or otherwise—can lead to a kind of fatigue. The essay examines how relentless winning might shape public sentiment, media narratives, and the broader social atmosphere.

It challenges the assumption that winning is always satisfying, suggesting that too much success can create its own set of complex emotions and reactions. The author encourages readers to consider the psychological and cultural consequences of a winning streak that never seems to end.

As part of this week's handpicked selection, this post offers a fresh perspective on how we experience and interpret success in the public sphere.
