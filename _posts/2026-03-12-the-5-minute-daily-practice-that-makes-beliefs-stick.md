Most people read books about beliefs, underline a few passages, and then...nothing changes.

This happens not because they lack discipline but because old beliefs are reinforced daily—under stress, under pressure, and even in familiar environments. When we’re tired or distracted, we revert to the interpretations we’ve practiced the longest.

Belief change requires repetition, evidence, and even a bit of structure.

That’s why I created the *Beyond Belief 30-Day Belief Transformation Journal*. I didn’t want *Beyond Belief* to be something you nod along with and forget. I wanted it to become practice.

The journal alternates between two types of work:

**🌞 Daily 5-Minute Practice**

These are quick morning and evening check-ins designed to gather evidence, set intentions, and track belief shifts.

Morning (5 minutes):

1. Identify yesterday's limiting belief
2. Flip it into a liberating belief (What could you believe instead?)
3. Rate both beliefs (0-10 scale)
4. Set your Three Powers intentions:
   - 👁 SEE: What will you notice today?
   - ⚡ FEEL: What do you expect?
   - 🎯 DO: What small action will you take?

Evening (5 minutes):

- Compare intended vs. actual
- Re-rate both beliefs
- Reflect on what created the shift

**✍️ In-Depth Exercises**

These are 30-60 minute exercises that build specific skills:

- identifying limiting beliefs,
- training attention,
- reworking relationship dynamics, and
- setting goals effectively.
