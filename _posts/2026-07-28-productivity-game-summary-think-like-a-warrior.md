**"Tough times never last and champions recognize that." – Darrin Donnelly**

In this summary, you'll learn a mindset from **five great coaches** that leads to unstoppable success.

**Favorite Takeaway:**

*Stop wasting effort reliving the past or worrying about the future. And drop any attitude of entitlement, believing you should already be further along than you are. Vow to stay humble and start a daily practice to take your mind off everything you can't control. Every night, ask yourself:*

1. *Did I give my fullest attention to my work today?*
2. *Did I bring the best possible attitude while doing it?*
