As part of my [Foundations](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/07/02/foundations-for-good-life/) project, I read 102 books over the past twelve-and-a-half months. That includes seven textbooks and dozens of academic books, in addition to the popular science and self-help books I selected for each theme.

Here are the most interesting things I learned this year while reading 102 books:

## On Fitness

1. **Exercise is the most powerful longevity drug.** Regular strength training and cardiovascular exercise reduce the risk of chronic disease, improve mood, and even protect cognitive function. *When* is your liver detox? Every night during sleep.
