## Bonus Insights from *Tiny Experiments*

**Bonus Insight #1: Learn in Public**

Whatever tiny learning experiment you commit to—writing a newsletter for 12 weeks, coding an app for 30 days, or producing a 20-second video every day for the next 20 days—publicly commit to that experiment and share your struggles, breakthroughs, and insights with people in real-time, either through a shared Google Doc with friends or a social media community.

By sharing your lessons as they happen, you invite others to follow you on your journey. (Content continues...)
