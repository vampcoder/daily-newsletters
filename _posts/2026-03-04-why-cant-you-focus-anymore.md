In this episode of *Solved*, Mark and Drew break down what's actually going on when you "lose focus."

We get into why the so-called attention crisis is probably overstated, how your brain is constantly negotiating between staying on task and switching to something else, and why boredom and distraction aren't moral failures—they're signals. We also talk about what flow states actually are, why they're rarer than people think, and how your environment is quietly shaping your attention far more than your willpower ever could.

If you've ever felt scattered, overstimulated, or frustrated with your inability to stay on one thing, this episode will change how you see it. Because "just concentrate" is actually terrible advice. And once you understand why, focus stops feeling like something you have to force.
