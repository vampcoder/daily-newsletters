# Follow the Money

The Bulwark's Tim Miller and Sam Stein break down the market's reaction to Trump's tariff announcements on Canada and Mexico, and what it says about the new political economy. They examine the numbers behind the volatility—Dow, S&P, and Treasury yields—and what they reveal about investor confidence.

They also turn to the growing influence of Elon Musk in the administration, and the questions it raises about who really holds power in this moment. As the conversation shows, markets are not just economic indicators; they are a lens into political reality.

This is a conversation about where the money is actually moving—and what that tells us about the decisions being made at the highest levels of government.
