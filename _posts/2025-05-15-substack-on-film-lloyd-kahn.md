# Lloyd Kahn at 90: Curiosity, Gardening, and the Beauty of the Bay

The nonagenarian writer and builder Lloyd Kahn shares his thoughts in a short film (7 minutes) on curiosity, gardening, and the beauty of the Bay.

In this intimate portrait, Kahn reflects on a life devoted to craft, nature, and the pursuit of knowledge. With characteristic humility and wit, he connects the simple acts of tending a garden and building by hand to a deeper philosophy of living well.

The film offers a gentle reminder that creativity and wonder don't fade with age—they deepen. Set against the backdrop of the Bay Area, it's a meditation on place, purpose, and the rewards of staying engaged with the world.
