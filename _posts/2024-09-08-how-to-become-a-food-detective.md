Avoiding glucose spikes was the beginning of my healing. And it may very well be the beginning of yours as well.

So, imagine looking at a dish, a menu, an item at the grocery store... and instantly knowing whether the food is going to keep your glucose steady (yay!), or create a glucose spike (nay!). Wouldn't that be super powerful?

It would. And I want to help you get there, so you can get agency and power back over your health.

Starches, sugars, protein, fat, and fiber are the molecules to know about.

- Starches and sugars turn to glucose when we digest them, and increase our glucose levels. They can cause glucose spikes (we want to minimize spikes to feel our best.)
- Protein, fat, and fiber keep our glucose levels steady. They should be the core of what we eat.

I've put together a [Food Classification Master List](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01J78VKHY7PB1ASE1CXYN86QR4_1) that will help you learn which foods contain which molecules. (Click it to expand and download it.)

Now, we can merge this with my 10 core glucose hacks to know when to eat which molecules for steady glucose. Knowing this will change everything about how you feel and how healthy your body is.

- **Breakfast:** focus on protein and fat. Plus, some fiber if you want. A little bit of starch for taste is ok, and no sugars, except whole fruit if you want, for taste.
- **Veggie starters:** fiber is essential. Protein and fat are good additions.
- **Main dishes:** choose a balance that favors protein, fat, and fiber, with moderate complex starches and minimal sugary additions.

By becoming a food detective, you can reclaim control over your glucose and feel your best every day.
