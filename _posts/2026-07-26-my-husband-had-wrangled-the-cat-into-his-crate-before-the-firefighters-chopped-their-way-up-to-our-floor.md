This week, we're breaking teeth, writing books, and going on our own odysseys…. But first, a story: The firefighters came for our cat. Sirens, lights, boots on the stairs. Small gray beast wedged behind the washer, refusing to move. My heart pounding like a drum solo. They coaxed, I prayed, the cat yawned. An hour later, they left with smiles and a story of their own. And in that chaos, a strange calm settled over our house—a reminder that even when the day goes sideways, we find our footing. Here's to the unexpected heroes, the stubborn companions, and the quiet after the storm.
