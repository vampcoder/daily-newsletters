Hello angels,

Today let’s talk about WATER.

First things first: yes, you should drink water. (News flash 😂)

55 to 60% of your body is water, and you gotta keep that amount up.

Around two litres per day is the usual recommendation. And as we age, we can feel less thirsty, so it’s important to make a point of drinking enough.

BUT there is a common question of whether you should drink water **during your meals**, or avoid it while you’re eating.

Let’s be clear: the studies are very small and there is no DEFINITIVE evidence, we need to do more research. But a few of these papers looked at water during a meal and the impact on glucose spikes. ([Study 1](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01J82X76ZSPKS24742SSRA2405_2), [Study 2](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01J82X76ZSPKS24742SSRA2405_3))

One [2018 study](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01J82X76ZSPKS24742SSRA2405_4) in particular did a test with a doughnut, showing that eating a doughnut AND drinking water at the same time increased the glucose spike of the doughnut.

I was able to replicate this easily on my own glucose monitor as well:

[Water after and during meal](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01J82X76ZSPKS24742SSRA2405_5)

Conclusion: there is preliminary evidence that it could be helpful to reduce how much water we drink **during meals**.

Personally, I’ve been trying to focus on chewing my food more, and I don’t limit how much water I drink. But I’ve noticed that more chewing has led to a bit less drinking.
