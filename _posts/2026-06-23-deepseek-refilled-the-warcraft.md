Good Morning, AI Enthusiasts!

AI got cheap enough to fake a living world, noisy enough to kill old code, and expensive enough to break its own business model.

**FUTURE**

DeepSeek Refilled the Warcraft

👀 What’s happening: DeepSeek has released a new model that undercuts existing pricing, forcing competitors to respond. The move makes AI more accessible but also puts pressure on companies that rely on high-margin AI services.

Why it matters: As AI becomes cheaper, the barrier to entry drops, enabling smaller startups to build sophisticated applications. However, this also means that companies must find new ways to differentiate beyond just model capability.

**PLUS**

Tesla Now Sells the Pick

Tesla's Cybertruck has finally hit the market, bringing its unique design and electric powertrain to the pickup truck segment. This could disrupt traditional truck manufacturers and accelerate EV adoption in a segment that has been slow to electrify.

Frontier Labs Can't Find Profit

Despite raising significant funding, Frontier Labs has yet to turn a profit. The company's high R&D costs and competitive pricing pressures highlight the broader challenges facing AI startups that are burning through cash to stay ahead.
