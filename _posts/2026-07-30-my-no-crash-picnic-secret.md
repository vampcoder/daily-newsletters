A few of you have asked me about picnic foods lately, Amol.

And I understand why. Picnics can make steady glucose feel surprisingly difficult. The crisps, bread, sandwiches, crackers, juices, cakes and biscuits... So today, let me tell you my **4 top tips** to enjoy picnic days without spending the rest of the afternoon on a glucose rollercoaster.

**#1. I make sure to pack some veggies**

Before anything else, I nibble on a few crudités. Some easy ideas for you:

- cherry tomatoes
- carrot sticks
- cucumber slices
- radishes
- baby gem leaves
- raw broccoli stem (yes, it's super good even raw!)

Whatever I have on hand, with some extra dips like hummus or tzatziki if I feel like it. Starting with veggies is the easiest way to flatten the glucose curve of everything that follows.

**#2. I build the main dish around protein and healthy fats**

This is the part that keeps my energy steady all afternoon, with no 4pm slump and no snack attack on the way home. Easy options:

- boiled eggs (they can be cooked the day before and put in the fridge overnight)
- hard cheeses
- chicken leftovers
- tinned tuna or sardines
- turkey slices
- nuts & olives
- avocado
- tinned lentils
- roasted chickpeas
- some good quality charcuterie

When I prep my picnic ahead of time, I love to make cold salads with lentils as a base, adding whatever protein and veggies I have in my fridge.
