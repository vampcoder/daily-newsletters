**Study #1: PCOS has a new name**

It is now officially called PMOS: Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome. This was decided by a big [international scientific consensus](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KWYDA7KY55SE9FPSBJFNME0J_1), and the new name carries a completely different message.

For years, PCOS was understood as primarily a reproductive condition: something that affected your periods, your fertility, your ovaries. The new name, PMOS, reflects what the science has actually been telling us for a while: this is a ***whole-body* metabolic condition**. Deeply connected to insulin resistance. Deeply connected to glucose. (As I mention in my previous YouTube video about PCOS).

That shift matters enormously. Millions of women have been managing symptoms like irregular cycles, acne, weight changes, facial hair, fatigue, and anxiety, without ever addressing the metabolic root driver: over 85% of women with PMOS symptoms have too much insulin.
