Does the future of every Indian depend on equity markets? Are Indians finally owning India's growth story? In a nation with over a billion dreams, what's the role that wealth creators will play?

In an all-new episode of the Create Wealth podcast, **Navneet Munot, MD and CEO of HDFC AMC and Chairman of AMFI,** answers these questions and more.

Navneet believes the answers to these questions lies in greater participation of the masses in equity markets. He shares how back in 2016 - 2017, SIP investments were about INR 4000 crores a year and at present the SIP investments have crossed INR 4000 crores a month!

This amazing growth in mutual fund investments is a true reflection of the growth of the Indian equity markets.

In fact, **HDFC AMC, under Navneet's leadership, manages a staggering INR 7 trillion –** more than the GDP of our neighbour Sri Lanka and 100 other countries combined!

Navneet's unique perspective on the pivotal role capital markets play not just in wealth creation but also in securing an inclusive, prosperous future for every Indian, makes this episode a very interesting watch!

**In this insightful post-election dialogue, we explore** –

* How domestic investors are now "owning the India growth story"
* The need for greater public participation in equity to secure retirement
* The shift from FPIs driving markets to domestic money calling the shots
* The wealth destruction caused by the gambling mindset in F&O trading
* The formula of wealth creation - ΔW=f(S,T,P)
* How affluent investors are creating wealth differently from retail investors
* What it takes to run a successful investment management company

This episode is a masterclass for wealth creators in India.
