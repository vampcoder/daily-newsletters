> **“The illiterate of the twenty-first century will not be those who cannot read and write, but those who cannot learn, unlearn, and relearn.” — Alvin Toffler**

---

In the following summary, you'll discover three surprisingly effective learning strategies.

**Favorite insight:**

*When you learn two or more loosely related skills simultaneously, those skills cross-pollinate because the insights you gain learning one skill lead to breakthroughs in the other. For instance, understanding tempo and time signatures while drumming can help you grasp rhythmic patterns while playing the guitar. When you go between practicing one skill and another, you bring fresh perspectives to your practices. If you spend a day practicing pickleball and then go back to practicing tennis, your pickleball perspective can help you see tennis in a new way, which can generate insights and help you escape a learning plateau. Multi-learning similar skills will also help you understand the nuances of any one skill. If you go between learning German and Spanish, you can easily contrast the two languages and quickly spot the unique qualities of each language.*
