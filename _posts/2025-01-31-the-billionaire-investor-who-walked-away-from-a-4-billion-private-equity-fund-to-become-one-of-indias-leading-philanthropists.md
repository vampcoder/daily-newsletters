In the latest episode of the Create Wealth Podcast, I talk to Ashish Dhawan—a name that needs no introduction in India’s investment and philanthropy circles.

From building one of India’s leading private equity firms, ChrysCapital, to walking away from it all, Ashish has chosen to channel his efforts towards philanthropy and nation-building.

The first time I met Ashish, a decade ago, I was in awe of his accomplishments as an investor. Since then, Ashish’s journey has been nothing short of inspirational.

Today, Ashish is one of the most revered and respected individuals in the development and education sectors. While his sharp investment acumen remains unshakeable, his passion for creating impact takes most of his time now.

Not only is he one of India’s top philanthropists, but through the Accelerate India Philanthropy venture, he’s also helping those with the means to relook at philanthropy and “give more, give sooner, and give better.”

Ashish is at the forefront of some of India’s most impactful institutions. He founded Ashoka University, one of India’s leading educational institutions. Through the Central Square Foundation and The Convergence Foundation, Ashish has been tackling India’s most critical challenges in the education and economic reforms segments.

He’s also one of the only Indians on the board of the Gates Foundation, shaping its strategy for economic growth, equal opportunity, and sustainability.

Ashish, with his clarity of thought, strong values, and positive outlook to nation-building, shares a treasure trove of insights in our conversation.
