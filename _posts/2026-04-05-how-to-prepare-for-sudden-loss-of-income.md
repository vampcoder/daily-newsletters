The gap between what you have and what you need is wider than you think.

Let me start with a simple question — if your income stops today, how long can you sustain your lifestyle without borrowing or selling assets?

Most people underestimate their fixed costs. They don't account for insurance premiums, EMIs, school fees, or annual vacation budgets. The result? The emergency corpus they've saved is often far smaller than what they'd actually need.

Here’s how to prepare for a sudden loss of income:

### 1. Calculate your fixed costs

First, list all your fixed expenses. Include not just essentials like rent and groceries, but also EMIs, insurance premiums, children's school fees, and even annual expenses like vacations or vehicle maintenance. Be honest — this is your true monthly burn rate.

### 2. Build a 12-month corpus

Most experts suggest a 6-month emergency fund. But in India, where finding a new job can take time and social security is limited, aim for at least 12 months of fixed costs. This corpus is your buffer against uncertainty.

### 3. Invest it wisely

An emergency corpus isn't meant to sit idle in a savings account. Park it in liquid instruments like liquid mutual funds, sweep-in FDs, or ultra-short-term funds. These offer better returns while keeping your money accessible within a few days.

### 4. Review your insurance cover

Ensure you have adequate health and term insurance. A medical emergency or unexpected liability can derail even the best financial plan. Review your policies annually to match your current lifestyle and goals.

### 5. Cut non-essential costs

Take a hard look at subscriptions, dining out, and impulse purchases. Redirect that money toward your emergency corpus. Small cuts add up faster than you think.

### 6. Create an additional income stream

Finally, build a side income — freelance work, consulting, or a small business. Even a modest extra income can be a lifeline during a job loss. It also accelerates your savings.

Remember, the goal is to close the gap between what you have and what you need. Start today, even if it’s small. Your future self will thank you.
