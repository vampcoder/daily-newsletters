Earlier this week, we hosted the Dezerv Wealth Summit in Bengaluru, in partnership with Moneycontrol. Over 350 professionals, founders, CXOs, and investors gathered to hear from some of India's top industry leaders on topics ranging from market cycles to ESOPs to where returns may come from next.

Here are the key insights from the summit:

- **Market cycles**: Understanding where we are in the market cycle is critical. Cycles are driven by a mix of economic fundamentals, sentiment, and liquidity. Investors who recognize the stage often make better decisions than those who try to time the market.

- **ESOPs**: Employee stock ownership plans can be life-changing, but they are not without pitfalls. Experts stressed the importance of early financial planning, understanding tax implications, and diversifying away from concentrated company stock.

- **Where returns come from next**: Many leaders believe that the next leg of returns will not come from the obvious, crowded trades. Instead, they see opportunities in underappreciated sectors, defensives, and through a disciplined, long-term asset allocation approach.

The summit reinforced that building wealth is not about chasing the next hot tip—it's about staying informed, staying disciplined, and staying patient.
