In my mid-20s, my parents convinced me to buy a house. They insisted I should have my own house before my family expands. Like a diligent son, I followed their advice—invested all my savings, took on a substantial home loan, and became a homeowner of a 2 bhk in Chembur, Mumbai.

Fortunately, things worked out well. But looking back, I know I made a decision that I would not have naturally made. Maybe, I would have invested more in mutual funds at that age. Yet I never questioned their advice or explained my own financial thinking.

We often make financial decisions based on family expectations rather than our personal financial strategy. And more importantly, we rarely have honest conversations about money with the very people whose opinions matter most to us.

A few weeks ago, we unpacked one of the toughest conversations in any family—*how to talk to your kids about money.*
