Welcome to the latest edition of the Create Wealth newsletter.

India's beauty and skincare industry is undergoing a remarkable renaissance. The sector has evolved beyond a one-size-fits-all approach, catering to diverse consumer needs and empowering individuals with choice and confidence. No longer hindered by distribution challenges, homegrown brands are now thriving through D2C models, redefining beauty standards and captivating consumer expectations and aspirations across the country.

This edition highlights the ascent of these innovative brands that are leading the charge in India's beauty revolution, transitioning the industry from a focus on insecurity and fear to one rooted in self-assurance and celebration of individuality.

Some noteworthy developments, like Bombay Shaving Company’s INR 24 crore fund-raise through Alteria Capital and Plum’s 71.3% revenue growth in FY23, caught our attention, and we decided to cover the Indian beauty and personal care (BPC) market in this edition.

We focus on the growth of India’s BPC industry by exploring successful business models, fundraises, and financials.

We cover:

* A snapshot of the BPC market in India
* How India's new-age beauty brands like mCaffeine, Mamaearth, Plum, and Ustraa are taking the fight to established, well-distributed legacy brands
* Key trends and insights
* What the future holds for India’s BPC market

**A snapshot of the BPC market in India**

India is the 4th largest beauty market in the world, with a market size of USD 19 billion (INR 1.98 trillion) in 2022, according to Redseer Strategy Consultants.

In India, the BPC products within the growing direct-to-consumer (D2C) segment have claimed the top spot in sales, as indicated by GoKwik's data.

It is projected to reach USD 30 billion by 2027, accounting for roughly 5% of the global BPC market.

The report expects India to emerge as a shining star with the highest compounded annual growth rate (CAGR) of 10% 2022-27) versus other comparable countries (the next best are Indonesia at 8% and China at 7%).

It is worth noting that online channels are significant contributors to the BPC segment in India and are projected to become a $10 billion market by 2027, accounting for about 33% of the Indian BPC segment by then.
