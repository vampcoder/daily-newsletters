# The AI revolution is here. Will the economy survive the transition?

The man who predicted the 2008 crash, Anthropic’s co-founder, and a leading AI podcaster jump into a Google doc to debate the future of AI—and, possibly, our lives.

The debate is led by Michael Burry, Dwarkesh Patel, and Anthropic’s co-founder. They discuss whether the AI revolution will be a net positive or a net negative for the economy and society.

**The Optimistic View**

Some participants argue that AI will lead to a future of abundance, where productivity soars and humans are freed from mundane work. They point to historical precedents where technological revolutions ultimately created more jobs than they destroyed.

**The Pessimistic View**

Others caution that the transition could be brutal, with massive job displacement outpacing the creation of new roles. They worry about widening inequality and the concentration of wealth in the hands of AI owners.

**The Pragmatic View**

A middle path suggests that governments and institutions must proactively manage the transition with policies like universal basic income and robust retraining programs. The outcome will depend on our collective choices.

**Key Quotes**

> "The AI revolution is not a time to be complacent. It is a time to be proactive." — Dwarkesh Patel
