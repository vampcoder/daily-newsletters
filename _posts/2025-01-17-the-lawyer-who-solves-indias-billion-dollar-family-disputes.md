New episode alert! In the latest episode of the Create Wealth podcast, I spoke to Rishabh Shroff — India's most influential private client lawyer and a partner at Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas.

As a trusted advisor to billionaires, family offices, and some of the country’s largest corporations, Rishabh has spent decades at the epicentre of high-stakes disputes, complex negotiations, and the intricate world of wealth and legacy planning.

Sitting in his office in Lower Parel, Mumbai, surrounded by art curated by his wife, Rishabh shared his experience of balancing the fine line between being a fierce litigator and a trusted confidante. This conversation was packed with stories, strategies, and lessons from India's wealthiest families that most of us have only read about in headlines.

In this episode, Rishabh opens up about:

- Behind-the-scenes stories of high-profile family disputes like Godrej and Tata-Mistry
- Why India's wealthiest families need private client lawyers beyond just disputes
- What it takes to become a lawyer in India’s leading law firms
- The career trajectories and compensation structures at top law firms (senior equity partners can earn ₹8–12 crores annually!)
- The untold story of his own family — Amarchand Mangaldas' separation

As we spoke, what struck me was how deeply personal his work is. Behind every trust, will, or succession plan lies a story — of family dynamics, legacies, and even unresolved conflicts. And only those with the knowledge, experience and empathy can be entrusted with this responsibility.

If you’ve ever wondered what happens behind the closed doors of India's wealthiest families, this episode offers rare insight.
