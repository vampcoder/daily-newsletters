For my second book, the *Glucose Goddess Method*, I ran an experiment in which 2,700 people followed my 4 most important hacks for 4 weeks. And this is what happened:

- 90% of people were less hungry all the time
- 89% of people cut their cravings
- 77% of people had more energy
- 67% of people were happier
- 58% of people slept better
- 58% of people improved their mental health
- 46% of people improved their skin
- 41% of people improved their diabetes
- 35% of people improved their hormones
- 99% of people created habits for life
