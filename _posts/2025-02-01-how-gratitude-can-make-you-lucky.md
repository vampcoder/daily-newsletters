Some skeptics may roll their eyes at the notion of "luck". But luck is made up of factors firmly grounded in observation: avoiding local optima, increasing our exposure, accessing the right information, and simply improving our outlook. These are all more mundane than mystical, and they all increase our chances of beneficial outcomes coming our way.
