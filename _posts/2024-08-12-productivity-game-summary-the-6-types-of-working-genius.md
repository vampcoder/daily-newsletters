> **"Teams and organizations that help people tap into their God-given talents are much more successful and productive than those that don’t." - Patrick Lencioni**

In this book summary, you’ll learn how to identify the six types of genius and build a high-performing, highly engaged, and highly fulfilled team.

**Favorite insight:**

*Everyone you work with is a genius in some way.*

If you give a teammate work that aligns with their innate genius, they’ll not only be more productive but also love what they do and remain loyal to your team. However, if you give them tasks that don’t align with their genius, they’ll be on the fast track to burnout. Your job as a leader is to discover people’s working genius types and give them work that leverages their strengths. Most people excel at two working genius types, are competent in another two, but find the remaining two frustrating and draining. No person can excel at all six working types.
