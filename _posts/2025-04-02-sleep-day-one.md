## What I’m doing for sleep

Sleep is one of those things where the fundamentals are well-known, but execution is hard. The main ideas I’m focusing on this month:

- **Consistency**: Going to bed and waking up at the same time every day, even on weekends.
- **Environment**: Keeping the bedroom dark, quiet, and cool.
- **Daytime habits**: Getting sunlight early in the morning, exercising regularly, and avoiding caffeine after noon.
- **Evening routine**: Avoiding screens for at least an hour before bed, and skipping heavy meals and alcohol late in the evening.
- **Tracking**: Using a sleep tracker to see how different habits affect sleep quality.

I’ll be reading a few books on sleep to go deeper. I’ll update again at the end of the month.
