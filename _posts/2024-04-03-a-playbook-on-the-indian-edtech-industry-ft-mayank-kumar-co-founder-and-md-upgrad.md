In the past few years, amidst the hyper-competitive and challenging ed-tech space, we have seen a true test of solid and sustainable businesses.

In the race to profitability and success, edtech companies have veered towards marketing and sales instead of focusing on building an education business.

With a keen interest in the edtech industry and the recent news about companies in this space, I was curious to know what it takes to build a successful edtech business.

In the latest episode of the Create Wealth podcast, I talk to Mayank Kumar, Co-founder and Managing Director of upGrad as he delivers an inside perspective on the edtech industry in India. What blew my mind was Mayank’s nuanced understanding of edtech and his mental models to build an edtech company from the ground up.

Mayank, the driving force behind the success of upGrad, completed his graduation from IIT Delhi followed by an MBA from ISB Hyderabad. In 2015, he co-founded upGrad and steadily built it to an ed-tech giant with a $2.25 billion valuation.

upGrad started with a cold start problem of people not going for higher education once they start working. They noticed a huge gap between people wanting to upskill themselves for better growth in their career but not finding the right platform and resources; that’s where upGrad found their product proposition.

**In this episode, we discuss -**

* How to build an edtech business in India
* The reason why there is a negative sentiment around the edtech industry
* Why no one wants to pay for their own education
* How upGrad tackled challenges during the Covid-pandemic
* How to solve for the user vs. buyer dichotomy
* Finding the unique product proposition in your business
* How to leverage ESOPs for wealth creation
* How to decide ESOP plan for employees
