How the Agarwal family built a ₹15,000 crore coal empire from nothing

This week’s Create Wealth podcast episode takes us to Indore—home to one of India’s largest coal importers, **Agarwal Coal Corporation**.

Tapan Agarwal’s family lost everything during Partition. His father started again with a single truck, and over four decades built a ₹15,000 crore business moving over **20 million tonnes of coal every year**.

Now, the second generation is writing its own chapter.

In our conversation, Tapan walked me through the family’s journey—from transport to coal, and now a bold ₹5,000+ crore bet on Indore real estate. We spoke about the second-gen challenge of balancing legacy with risk, and how a family office helps protect wealth at scale.

And then there’s his other identity: *Car Crusader*.
