It’s with great joy, excitement, and gratitude, that I present to you my new book, **9 Months That Count Forever: How your pregnancy diet shapes your baby’s future**.

*9 Months That Count Forever* was born from the most vulnerable and powerful year of my life. When I became pregnant, I thought I understood what that meant: positive pregnancy test, wait 9 months, birth. But then I discovered a world of science – a field called Developmental Origins of Health and Disease – that changed everything. I realized I wasn’t just carrying my son: I was shaping the foundation of his brain, his immune system, his future health and strength ***for life***, with **what I was eating during these 9 months**. What??? oh my gosh. That knowledge felt like a superpower. But it also made me angry. Why wasn’t anyone telling me this? Why is this life-changing science not something every parent hears the moment they find out they’re expecting?

I needed to put it all in this book. This information is for anyone who wants to give a baby the strongest start.
