For many of us, coffee is more than a drink—it’s a ritual that fuels our day. But did you know that your coffee habits might be impacting your glucose levels? In this episode, I uncover how coffee affects our bodies and share 3 science-backed tips to help you enjoy your favorite brew without unexpected glucose spikes.

And here’s a little secret: I do a coffee detox once in a while to reset my body and stay in tune with how caffeine affects me. In this episode, I’ll share how you can enjoy coffee and keep it working for you, not against you.

Here’s what you’ll learn:

- **Coffee & Type 2 Diabetes** – Is coffee helping or hurting when it comes to your long-term health?
- **Stress, Coffee, & Glucose** – How caffeine and stress can combine to impact glucose.
- **Coffee and Insulin** – What you need to know about coffee's effect on insulin sensitivity.
- **Sugar, Sweeteners & Spikes** – How a simple sugar cube or sweetener can change your morning coffee’s impact.
- **The Best Milk for Coffee** – From oat to almond to dairy, find out which option works best for glucose control.

So whether you prefer a black coffee, a sweetened latte, or your favorite oat milk cappuccino, I’ll show you simple hacks to keep your coffee habit both enjoyable and healthy.
