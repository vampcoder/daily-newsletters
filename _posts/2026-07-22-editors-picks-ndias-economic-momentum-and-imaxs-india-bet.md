India's economic momentum is picking up pace, and global companies are taking notice. Among the latest to double down on the market is IMAX, which is making a significant bet on India's growing appetite for premium entertainment.

The country's economic trajectory has become a focal point for investors, driven by a young population, digital adoption, and policy reforms that are reshaping the business landscape. As India's middle class expands, demand for both essential and discretionary services continues to rise.

For IMAX, the bet is on India's theatrical market, which is gradually rebounding and showing a preference for large-format, immersive experiences. The company's strategy reflects a broader confidence in India's consumer market potential over the long term.

This edition of Editor's Picks brings together a selection of commentary and analysis on India's economic momentum and the strategic moves being made by global players.
