# Stop Confusing Your Dreams with Your Goals

Knowing the difference makes you more likely to accomplish both.

My daughter Jasmine and I were on a six-hour road trip, and all I could think about was my goals for the *Beyond Belief* book launch. I kept fixating on hitting the *New York Times* bestseller list, running an incredible speaking tour, and helping the lives of millions. Jasmine listened for a while, then pointed out that none of those things were up to me. I didn’t have goals—I had dreams.

Three books on behavior change, and I had missed something that basic.

This week’s article is the framework we built based on that conversation. You’ve probably heard of SMART goals: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound. WISE goals ask a question SMART never does: is this within my control?

Jasmine and I turned that road trip conversation into a simple test you can run on any goal in about thirty seconds. We break down the difference between goals and dreams, show where SMART goals fall short, and give you a new model to rewrite your own goals before the week is out.
