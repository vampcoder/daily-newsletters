Hello angels,

For years, you’ve asked me: “Jessie, what protein powder do you recommend?”

And for years, I never had a satisfying answer.

Not because protein isn’t important. It’s very important (in fact, it’s one of the nutrients I think most people need more of). The problem was the powders: the hundreds I tried felt like something you had to *tolerate* in exchange for the health benefits.

Chalky on my tongue. Gritty texture. Stuck to my teeth and to the bottom of the glass. Flavored with all sorts of artificial things to mask the taste of the protein itself. I realized everyone seemed to be solving the same problem: How do we make mediocre protein taste acceptable?

So I went back to first principles and started asking a different question:

**What if the protein itself were better?**

This quest led me down a rabbit hole that took years. What I discovered is that the secret isn’t what happens after the protein is made. It’s how the protein is collected from the milk in the first place.

Eventually, I found a first-of-its-kind technique called **cold cross-flow microfiltration**.

Here’s why it’s different: Most protein powders are filtered using heat, chemicals, or both. This process uses neither. No heat. No chemicals. Just a gentle filtration system that separates the protein while preserving it in 100% native form.

The result was unlike any protein powder I’d ever tried. Fine like flour. Silky to the touch. Delicate light milky taste. Nothing to hide.

And then came….. the ultimate test:

I added it to my morning coffee. Well, ladies and gentlemen: it frothed. It disappeared. No chalkiness, no weird aftertaste, just coffee and 20 grams of protein (that’s as much as in 3 eggs!).

And just like that, I solved something I had been thinking about for years: when I don’t have time to make a savoury breakfast, how can I turn my coffee into a protein-rich, satiating start to the day that keeps me steady and energized.
