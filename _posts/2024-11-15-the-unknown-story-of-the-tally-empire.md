How India’s first family-owned SaaS company built the most loved accounting software in India

New episode alert!

How did India’s first family-owned SaaS company build the most loved accounting software in India?

I clearly remember when my father, a Chartered Accountant, used Tally for the first time and told my brothers and me about its simplicity. A few years later, on weekends, I started learning how to use it.

Tally has been THE accounting software for Indians, and most small and medium Indian businesses have used it at some point in their journey!

What is more inspiring is that Tally had set standards for accounting software in India long before "SaaS" was even a concept.
