What if you could manage your wealth like the ultra-rich, achieving the perfect balance between security, lifestyle, and high-growth aspirations?

In the latest episode of the Create Wealth podcast, I’m joined by Dr. Aarti Gupta, Chief Investment Officer (CIO) of the DM Gupta Family Office and Anikarth Ventures. We delve into the sophisticated strategies used by ultra-wealthy families to manage, preserve, and grow their wealth across generations.

We discuss how family offices differ from traditional institutional investors, and the role that alternative investments play in today’s market. We explore the concept of a total portfolio approach—how having a clear understanding of capital requirements and risk tolerance allows for both stability and aggressive growth opportunities.

Dr. Gupta also shares insights on evaluating private equity and venture capital opportunities, and how regular investors can adopt certain principles from the family office playbook, like maintaining a long-term perspective and focusing on downside protection.
