Dear Amol,

The sole focus of the Varsity team is to find new and meaningful ways to improve financial literacy among Indians. With this in mind, we started publishing articles and videos but have also launched several offline initiatives. Here’s a quick update on what we’ve been up to over the past few months.

1. We resumed our newsletter, Mind over Markets by Zerodha Varsity. Every fortnight we share our views on asset classes, investment approaches, financial planning, and everything related to finance and economics. You could check it out on [Substack](https://zerodhavarsity.substack.com/) or enroll to receive it in your inbox.
2. We also started [Side Notes by Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodhavarsity.substack.com/s/side-notes), a podcast on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/6tCBkWtxqjKOILJvSgREAA) and [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/side-notes-by-zerodha-varsity/id1778022935), and a fortnightly newsletter on business stories and stock analysis.
3. We concluded the latest Zerodha Varsity Community Meet-up in Chennai on 26th April 2025. The meet-up brought together a vibrant group of traders-investors for a day of thoughtful conversations on trading and investing amid today’s turbulent markets. Highlight: The meet-up in Chennai drew a larger female audience than any other Varsity meet-up so far.
4. The Chennai meet-up followed another successful meet-up in Pune on 13th February 2025, during which the community discussed mutual funds and personal finance.
5. The Zerodha Varsity quizzes in colleges across the country are becoming increasingly popular among students.
6. Our [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@varsitybyzerodha) channel recently crossed 5 lakh subscribers. And we also launched a [Zerodha Varsity Kannada](https://www.youtube.com/@ZerodhaVarsityKannada) channel.

Zerodha Varsity is on a mission to make financial education available to all — stay tuned!

Best,  
Team Varsity
