As mental health diagnoses surge, so does the reliance on therapy and medication.  

While these interventions can be helpful, they are reactive rather than preventive.  

To support children’s mental health, we need to focus on one of the root causes.  

What do children really need psychologically, and what does contemporary daily life fail to provide?
