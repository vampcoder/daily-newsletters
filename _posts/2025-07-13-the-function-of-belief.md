Your beliefs are one of the most powerful forces in your life. They are essential in helping you navigate and make sense of the world, and they shape your reality in a fundamental way.

So it can be easy to forget that your beliefs are tools, not truths. You have the power to choose your beliefs, and that means you can upgrade them when they’re no longer serving you.

What stories are you telling yourself about your limitations? Which of your beliefs energize you to take action, and which leave you feeling stuck? What would you attempt if you truly believed it was possible?

—

**💬 Join the Discussion**

Healthy self-reflection is active, curious, and future-oriented. Overthinking/Rumination is passive, self-critical, and stuck in the past.

How can we balance the fine line between the two?
