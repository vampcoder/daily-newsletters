## Editor's Picks: South make investment pitch, Flipkart to enter food delivery

### South India's Investment Pitch

South Indian states are stepping up their efforts to attract investment, presenting themselves as prime destinations for both domestic and foreign capital. The pitch emphasizes their strong infrastructure, skilled workforce, and business-friendly policies, aiming to position the region as a hub for manufacturing and technology.

### Flipkart to Enter Food Delivery

Flipkart is gearing up to enter the food delivery market, a move that is set to intensify competition in India's rapidly growing online food services sector. The company is expected to leverage its existing logistics network and customer base to challenge established players.
