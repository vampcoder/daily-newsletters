## Editor's Picks: South make investment pitch, Flipkart to enter food delivery

*July 24, 2026*

Moneycontrol cuts through the clutter and brings you the day's most important business stories. Here are the editor's picks.

### South makes investment pitch

Southern states are stepping up their efforts to attract both domestic and foreign investment. With competitive policies, infrastructure development, and a focus on emerging sectors, the region is positioning itself as a prime destination for capital. This coordinated pitch reflects a broader trend of regional governments actively competing for economic growth.

### Flipkart to enter food delivery

E-commerce giant Flipkart is gearing up to enter the food delivery space, a move that could disrupt the existing market. By leveraging its vast logistics network and customer base, Flipkart aims to carve out a significant share in the rapidly growing online food ordering segment. This expansion is expected to intensify competition among established players and reshape consumer choices.
