Hello angels,

Last week on Instagram I posted about having a high-protein shake before a big day of work. This keeps me full for many hours so it's perfect when I'm on the go.

So many of you requested the recipe, so here it is!!

**My Protein Shake Recipe:**

* **2 scoops** protein powder 🥄 (sometimes I even put 3 to up the protein)
* **2/3 cup** frozen berries 🍓
* **Handful of nuts** of your choice 🌰 (I like almonds, and if I think about it I'll soak them overnight in filtered water)
* **Whole milk or unsweetened nut milk** 🥛 (from half a cup to 2 cups, to your preferred creaminess)
* **Optional for added texture:** 1 tablespoon unsweetened nut butter 🥜

Blend and enjoy!
