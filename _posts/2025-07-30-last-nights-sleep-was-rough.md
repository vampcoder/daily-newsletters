Angels, I am *so sensitive* to **sleep**. Like I get soooo grumpy when I haven't slept enough. And I get really bad cravings. (so much harder to resist the mid-morning pain au chocolat!)

But it helps me to know that there's a scientific reason behind it: **when we don’t get enough sleep, our body struggles to manage glucose.** Yep, the same breakfast that’s usually fine can cause a bigger glucose spike after a bad night’s sleep. And a bigger glucose spike = less energy, more hunger, more cravings, and more “why did I just eat that entire jumbo bag of m&ms?” moments.

***So what do I do to feel better?*** I stick to my morning routine. Here are the hacks that help me:

✅ **I look out the window as soon as you wake up.** Natural light in our eyes first thing helps regulate your circadian rhythm and this in turn can help balance our glucose.

✅ **Move for 10 minutes.** A quick walk or dance break in the morning, especially after breakfast, helps our body regulate glucose.

✅ **Finish your shower with 30 seconds of cold water.** Cold exposure boosts your nervous system’s resilience to stress (and yes, sleep deprivation = stress!) and can reduce insulin resistance.
