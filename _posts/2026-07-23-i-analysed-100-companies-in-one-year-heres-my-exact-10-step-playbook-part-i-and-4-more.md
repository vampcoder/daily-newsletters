There is a very particular kind of quiet at 3:00 a.m. The ceiling fan hums. A door clicks shut somewhere down the corridor. The chai went cold an hour ago, and on the laptop screen, page 87 of a 316-page annual report is calmly winning a staring contest with me.

I’ve spent the last year dissecting over 100 companies—from scrappy startups to entrenched conglomerates—and along the way, I distilled my process into a 10-step playbook that cuts through the noise. This is Part I, where I break down the first half of the framework.
