The latest episode of *Solved* just dropped: [***Dating, Solved: Why Finding the “Right Person” Feels So Hard Now***](https://solvedpodcast.com/dating)

Modern dating is messy. Between the apps, the ghosting, and the endless advice online, it’s easy to overthink everything—your texts, your standards, your worth. But if you dig into what is behind all that overthinking, you’ll find the real issue: fear.

Fear of rejection, fear of not being enough, fear of getting hurt again.

In this episode, Drew and I break down the real psychology behind dating struggles, from unrealistic expectations to ego traps to attachment habits that keep you stuck. We talk about how to date with more clarity, courage, and self-respect… and without the games.

You’ll walk away with science-backed tools to approach dating with more confidence: how to spot your triggers, shift your mindset, and show up more fully in every stage of connection.
