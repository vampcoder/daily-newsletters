It's not laziness.

Most “time management” problems are really just fear management problems.

We tend to think that procrastination is a failure of discipline or willpower. We label ourselves lazy, unmotivated, or scattered. But the real culprit is often fear—fear of failure, fear of judgment, fear of uncertainty. The task isn't avoided because it's hard; it's avoided because it demands something from us that we're scared to give.

The next time you catch yourself putting something off, ask: What am I afraid of here? Often, the answer reveals that it's not the work itself, but the possibility of doing it imperfectly, or the risk of being seen. Once you name the fear, it loses much of its power.

So, stop calling it laziness. Call it what it is—fear—and then decide that you're not going to let it run the show.
