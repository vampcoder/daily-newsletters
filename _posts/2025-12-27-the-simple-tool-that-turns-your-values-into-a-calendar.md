If you’ve read *Indistractable*, you know I’m obsessed with timeboxing. It’s the single most effective way I’ve found to make sure my time matches my values. It’s also the difference between *hoping* you’ll work on what matters and actually doing it.

Here’s why: you can’t call something a distraction unless you know what it’s distracting you *from*. Without a plan for your day, you’re at the mercy of other people’s priorities: their emails, their requests, their “quick” chats. Timeboxing gives you a blueprint for your week so you can say “yes” and “no” with confidence.

To make it even easier, I’ve created a simple Schedule Maker you can use to visually map your ideal week. It’s a Google Sheet that helps you:

- **Translate values into time** so the things that matter most actually get space on your calendar.
- **Balance the three life domains**—you, relationships, and work—without letting one consume the others.
- **Spot and fix time leaks** that silently eat away at your focus and energy.
- **Create a reusable weekly template** you can adjust in minutes, not hours.

I use a version of this myself, and I revisit it every week to make small tweaks. It’s surprising how quickly you’ll see patterns like the meeting that always overruns, or the “quick break” that turns into an afternoon lost online. Once you see your time laid out visually, it’s much harder to fool yourself about where it’s going.
