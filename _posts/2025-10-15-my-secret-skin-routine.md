Confession: **last night was couch + blanket + Instagram.** Then this popped up in my notifications and made me laugh out loud.👇

And sorry in advance to disappoint, but I'm *not* going to talk about **a super secret daily skincare routine** with expensive serums today. Because you know what? Glow actually starts **from the inside**, with... you guessed it: **protein!**

Your body is like a **construction site that never sleeps.** Every minute, tiny factories in your cells *(called ribosomes)* snap together amino acids to make the proteins you run on. It’s wild, and it’s wonderfully simple: **when the building blocks are there, your body builds.**

**First, your skin.** Collagen and elastin are proteins, the scaffolding that gives skin its firmness and bounce.

**Then your hair.** It’s mostly keratin (*another protein ❤️*). When you eat less protein, your hair can get finer and more breakable. Hello split ends!

**Brain & immune system.** Many hormones, neurotransmitters, and antibodies are proteins too. Feeding your brain enough protein often means steadier mood, clearer focus, more resilience.

**Tone & posture.** Protein helps you maintain lean mass, and the quiet “lift” that keeps you upright as the years go by.
