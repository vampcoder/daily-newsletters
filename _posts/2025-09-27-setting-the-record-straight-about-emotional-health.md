# Setting the Record Straight About Emotional Health

Emotions aren't inconveniences to control. They're biological messengers we can't silence, only bury.

When parents choose to validate feelings instead of suppressing them, it helps children develop resilience rather than anxiety.

What if the feelings you've been taught to ignore are actually telling you what you need to hear most?
