Today's the day. *Beyond Belief* is officially out in the world. I'm feeling a mix of excitement, relief, and a whole lot of gratitude.

Seeing readers tag me in photos and share passages that landed has been overwhelming in the best way. If you've posted, shared, or sent a kind note, thank you. While I may not be able to respond to everyone, please know that I see you, and it means more than I can say.

**Here's what Terra, a real estate professional, shared recently:**

> *"I used the turnaround exercise before a difficult meeting with my boss. What was supposed to be a 30-minute confrontation turned into a 2-hour deep conversation that created a new foundation for our professional relationship.*
>
> *I actually shared the exercise with my boss and it really meant a lot to him that I was willing to reflect on the situation and take ownership.*
>
> *At the very least, I am so proud of how I showed up."*

**The belief Terra challenged:** "My boss doesn't respect me and doesn't value what I bring to the table."

**What happened:** What changed wasn't her boss. She examined her assumptions and reversed the belief. The entire professional relationship shifted.

That's the power of belief put into action.

Belief work only changes outcomes when it changes behavior. And behavior only shifts when beliefs are examined deliberately—and repeatedly.

That's why *Beyond Belief* isn't just a set of ideas. It's a framework for noticing, questioning, and redesigning the assumptions shaping your decisions.
