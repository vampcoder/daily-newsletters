**"Escape competition through authenticity." – Naval Ravikant**

Naval Ravikant is a modern philosopher and successful tech founder. His *How to Get Rich (Without Getting Lucky)* has been viewed 10 million times. The podcasts he appears on get millions of downloads.

Here's a video of his **best advice on building wealth** (the type of wealth that gives you the freedom to live life on your terms).

**Favorite Takeaway:**

To truly offer one-of-a-kind value you must grow your stack through self-learning. Whatever you learn in a classroom can be replicated and copied. Your advantage only grows through experimenting and self-learning. Not just any self-learning, but learning the skills of building and selling. Naval says, **"Learn to sell. Learn to build. If you can do both, you will be unstoppable."**
