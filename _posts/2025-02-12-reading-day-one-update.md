I’m just beginning the fifth month of my [year-long Foundations project](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/07/02/foundations-for-good-life/). The focus for this month is reading. In case you’re interested, here are the previous months’ content:

1. Fitness: [Start](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/10/01/foundations-fitness-day-1/), [End](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/10/31/fitness-month-end-update/), [Books](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/10/22/what-i-learned-about-getting-in-shape-after-reading-13-books-this-month/).
2. Productivity: [Start](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/11/06/productivity-opening-update/), [End](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/11/29/productivity-end/), [Books](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/11/19/10-important-books-productivity/).
3. Money: [Start](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/12/03/money-day-one/), [End](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/12/31/money-month-end/), [Books](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/12/17/10-books-money/).
4. Food: [Start](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2025/01/06/food-opening-update/), [End](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2025/02/04/food-month-end-update/), [Books](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2025/01/13/what-i-learned-and-unlearned-reading-10-books-on-nutrition/).

I picked this focus as one of the twelve because I believe a regular habit of reading, particularly from books, is one of the best foundational habits you can have in life. It’s also one that people do less frequently than they used to: most adults read fewer books than they did in decades past, no doubt a trend caused by the rise of smartphones.
