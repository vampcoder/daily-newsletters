# A Billion Bodies

Plus: AI Took A Seat At The G7, GPUs Are The New Exit

By Magna & Ben • 18 Jun 2026

---

Good Morning, AI Enthusiasts!

Bodies, governments, brands, and consultants are all just new surfaces to monetize.

---

## The New Surface Economy

In the era of ubiquitous AI, value is no longer confined to traditional assets. The newsletter's central thesis is that we are witnessing the rise of a "surface economy"—where every entity, from the individual human body to entire governments, becomes a potential monetization surface.

- **Bodies**: With AI-driven health tracking, biometric data, and personalized medicine, the human body has become a rich source of data and a target for hyper-personalized services.
- **Governments**: AI's integration into public services and governance is transforming states into platforms that can optimize, tax, and serve in new ways.
- **Brands**: Marketing and customer engagement are being reimagined through AI, turning every brand interaction into a measurable, monetizable event.
- **Consultants**: Even the consulting industry is being re-platformed by AI, with expertise being packaged, sold, and delivered through new digital surfaces.

---

## AI Takes A Seat At The G7

In an unprecedented move, AI has been granted a formal seat at the G7 table. This marks a historic shift in global governance, recognizing artificial intelligence as a key actor in shaping international policy on economics, security, and technology. The decision signals the urgency for a coordinated, international framework to manage AI's impact on the global order.

---

## GPUs Are The New Exit

The startup landscape is undergoing a tectonic shift. No longer is the initial public offering (IPO) or a traditional acquisition the only coveted exit strategy. Now, "GPU exits" are on the rise. This occurs when a startup's primary value is its accumulated computing power—its GPU clusters and AI training infrastructure. Larger players acquire these startups primarily for their hardware, effectively making compute the new currency and the definitive measure of a company's worth.
