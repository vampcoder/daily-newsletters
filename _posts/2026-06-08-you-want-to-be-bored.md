## One thing for you to think about

Everything that matters most in life will sometimes bore you.

Your marriage will bore you. Your career will bore you. Your kids will bore you.

But boredom isn’t a sign that you chose wrong. It’s a signal that you have chosen to make something more important than your own momentary pleasure.

It’s the hidden fee that comes with purpose and meaning.
