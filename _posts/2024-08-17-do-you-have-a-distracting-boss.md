Successful planning is that which contains the unexpected. Yet many workplaces are still filled with "crises": urgent fires that demand our immediate attention.

But how many of these fires are the product of poor prioritization from management? How often is the attention they're given simply the result of internal triggers?

People procrastinate because they don’t want to deal with the discomfort associated with doing the project, such as anxiety, uncertainty, or boredom.

Procrastination is not an inherent human trait. It’s an emotion-regulation problem.
