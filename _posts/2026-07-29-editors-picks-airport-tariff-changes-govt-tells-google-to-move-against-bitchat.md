### Airport Tariff Changes

The government has proposed significant changes to airport tariffs that could affect airline operating costs and, ultimately, passenger fares. This move is part of a broader effort to regulate infrastructure pricing while balancing the interests of airlines, airport operators, and consumers.

### Govt Tells Google to Move Against Bitchat

In a significant regulatory directive, the Indian government has asked Google to take action against the messaging app Bitchat. The move raises questions about platform accountability and the enforcement of local laws on global tech companies.
