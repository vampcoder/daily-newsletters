New episode alert!

From an Amazon engineer to founder of an INR 8,000 crore company – Gaurav Kushwaha, the founder of BlueStone, is rewriting the jewellery industry playbook.

In the latest episode of the Create Wealth podcast, I talk to Gaurav as he gives a masterclass on the jewellery industry and what it takes to build an INR 8,000 crore business.

What fascinated me the most?

His strategic approach to choosing the jewellery market wasn't about trends—it was about deep, structural advantages. Most saw a difficult market with well-established players. But Gaurav saw:

- A market larger than electronics
- Unique supply and demand dynamics
- Massive growth potential beyond traditional retail

So, what did this engineer who had zero experience in the jewellery industry and had never entered a jewellery shop do?

He went back to first-principles!

The result? A company that has challenged conventional wisdom and opened up an entire market.

Curious about how unconventional thinking can transform industries? Watch this episode now!

It is a goldmine of insights for founders and business owners who are searching for clarity on category selection, market dynamics, and scaling strategies.
