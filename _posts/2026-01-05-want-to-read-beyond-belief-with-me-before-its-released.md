For the past five years, I’ve been obsessed with one question:

**Why do smart, hardworking people struggle to follow through (even when they know what to do)?**

I used to think the answer was discipline. I was wrong. The more I studied it, the more the real culprit kept showing up: **beliefs**.

So I poured everything I’ve learned into a new book: *Beyond Belief* (out March 10, 2026). This book is by far the most important work I’ve ever done. Writing it challenged me to rethink everything I thought I knew about pain, perception, and human potential.

Whether it’s our relationships, our work, or our health, we all carry hidden limiting beliefs that keep us from living the life we deserve. *Beyond Belief* reveals the surprising new science of identifying and changing these limiting beliefs to have more fulfilling relationships, succeed in business, and even extend our lifespans by a whopping 7.5 years!
