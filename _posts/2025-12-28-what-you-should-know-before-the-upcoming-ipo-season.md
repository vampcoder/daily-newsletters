2025 has seen it all.

As the year wraps up, Indian markets have digested everything from sharp rallies to long consolidation phases, from record domestic flows to relentless supply. But if there is one part of the market that has refused to slow down, it is the IPO pipeline.

In the last 12 months alone, **344 companies have gone public, raising over ₹1.5 lakh crore**. New issues have become a weekly fixture. Oversubscription numbers are frequently hitting 50-100 times, making headlines and drawing in a wave of first-time investors.

With the new IPO season around the corner, it's worth asking: how should investors think about applying for IPOs?

While the excitement around new listings is understandable, the key to navigating a hot IPO market is to stay disciplined. Here are some important considerations:

- **Understand the company, not just the buzz.** Before applying, read the Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) to understand the business model, the industry, the financials, and the risks. A good IPO is one where the company is solid, not just where the listing day pop seems high.
- **Valuation matters.** In a strong market, IPOs are often priced aggressively. Make sure you are comfortable with the asking price relative to the company's earnings, growth, and peers.
- **Think long-term.** IPO investing is not just about listing gains. Ask yourself: would you hold this stock for the next five years? If the answer is no, it is better to stay away.

As always, past performance is not an indicator of future returns. The best way to approach an IPO is with a clear framework, patience, and a focus on quality — not just FOMO.

Here's to a season of informed decisions.
