Jessie here, the biochemist behind GlucoseGoddess. And this is the story of how I became so passionate about glucose.

At 19 years old, I had a terrible accident. I broke my back jumping off a waterfall. After an invasive surgery and lots of physical pain, it's my mental health that started deteriorating.

Depression, anxiety, dissociation, I got thrown into a state of constant fear. My body felt like a black box I couldn't understand, and no one around me was able to help me. I felt isolated, profoundly scared, and I just wanted to feel like myself again.

It became clear to me that my health was the #1 most important thing in my life.

And I had no other option but to go on a quest to try to get it back.
