Most people think aging starts with wrinkles. But inside your body, something else (far more important) is quietly aging: **your muscles**.

After 30, we start **losing up to 8% of our muscle mass every decade**. That means we lose nearly a third of our muscles by the time we retire! ([read study](https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01K77FPQR9S09X9CTQEBGRW6GX_2))  And this is not because we stop moving, but because our body becomes **less efficient at using protein**.

**And muscles aren't just about strength or looks**. They're your glucose regulators, your metabolic engines, your mood stabilizers, your joint protectors, your hormone support system, and more.  
And when our muscles **start to shrink**, everything else goes off balance: recovery slows, focus drops, posture weakens, and your mood, energy and sleep quality take a big hit.
